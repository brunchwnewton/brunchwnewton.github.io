Aethic reasoning, in order

1The thought experiment

Illustrative thought experiment on the Aethic “Big Three” principles

Alice and Bob drive in their car on the backroads. Alice, the driver, spots a hitchhiker on the side of the road, and mentions this to Bob. Bob (whose Aethus we will call “Bob before”), wonders which side of the road the hitchhiker is on, then immediately afterwards looks out the window. Upon seeing that the hitchhiker is on their side of the road, Bob transitions to the Aethus “Bob after”.

  1. The extrusion principle states that although relative to Bob after the hitchhiker is on their side of the road, relative to Bob before the hitchhiker is also in Aethic superposition even now of being on the opposite side of the road as well.
  2. The principle of centric unfolding states that Bob was equally likely at the moment of looking to have landed on an alternative after Aethus for which the hitchhiker was on the opposite side of the road from them, even though the hitchhiker would have been there before (in the proper time sense) the car got there and Alice1 noticed them.
  3. The union principle itself resolves a paradox that seems to appear here: if relative to Bob before the hitchhiker is indeed on both sides of the road, then why do the two multiverse-like copies of themselves not look across the road and see one another? The answer is stunning: to resolve this paradox while remaining consistent with the extrusion principle, we have to conclude that the mutual logical negations blotting out each version of the hitchhiker from the other reality are internal to those respective realities like a ledger entry, rather than an exterior timeline branch which physically separates them. Therefore, each of the two possible after Aethae to Bob, one for each hitchhiker position, explicitly declares that the hitchhiker being at the other position is false, thereby forming exactly the concept of an Aethic disagreeing superposition, for which both outcomes with their histories and all already physically exist in the same physical space (relative to Bob before that is) but internally enforce the logical negation of the other so as to emergently produce their empirical noninteraction. This also means that by simply refraining from stating the mutual negations, we construct the concept of an agreeing superposition, the kinematic powersets over which, upon Aethic filtering, is proposed to produce quantum mechanics proper.

Note 1, on Alice. Interestingly, whether Alice herself saw the hitchhiker on the left or right side of the road is in disagreeing superposition relative to Bob before, since he himself is not yet Aethically aware. So instead of naively defining “Alice’s Aethus” as her Aethus of the moment, instead we have to define “Alice right” and “Alice left”, and then conclude that Bob before holds both in superposition, specifically coupled as \(\textrm{Hitchhiker right} \times \textrm{Alice right} + \textrm{Hitchhiker left} \times \textrm{Alice left}\). The intuitive concept of “Alice’s Aethus” is therefore more like an equivalence class over these ontologically exact Aethae rather than one of them itself, (in the sense that we implicitly mean that we are referring to any one of them as the “single” Alice Aethus for that consideration). However, before seeing the hitchhiker herself, Alice at Alice before holds Alice right and Alice left in the same superposition that Bob before does (ie as a weighted parent Aethus of Alice before), and so following Alice’s own centric unfolding we reach that her consciousness will find itself with a random probability as either winding up in the Alice right or Alice left Aethus branch upon her looking. When Bob undergoes his own centric unfolding, he simply invalidates relative to him his joint Aethus with the version of Alice who got the opposite answer. Back

In the algebra

The footnote’s coupled sum is a product of states, read on the validity quotient. Alice’s record is unresolved for Bob, so it enters as the sum over its two alternatives; that her record is a record of the road makes the two mismatched combinations jointly invalid, and the quotient strikes them:

\[ \begin{aligned} &\pi\big((p\,H_R + q\,H_L)(A_R + A_L)\big) \\ &\qquad = p\,H_R A_R + q\,H_L A_L . \end{aligned} \]

When Bob looks and finds \(H_R\), multiplying by \(H_R\) strikes the remaining term, the version of Alice who got the opposite answer.

2The derivation

The two-tag deduction

The polarized double slit, walked from the postulates to both of its empirical faces: the analyzer’s readout probabilities and the screen’s intensity pattern. Every trigonometric factor is the Bloch face’s contribution and every \(x\)-dependence is spacetime’s; the elementary alternatives are their products, a spacetime route through one slit carrying a Bloch route to that slit’s tag.

Terms

slit
a spacetime opening, the spacetime arena’s alternative.
tag
the polarization stamp a slit’s polarizer imposes, a determinate node on the Bloch sphere indexed to that slit. The tags sit at \(\pm\alpha\) from the incident polarization, the bisector.
tag overlap
\(c = \cos 2\alpha\), how alike the two stamps are.
analyzer
the final polarizer at angle \(\theta\), read as a which-tag question; write \(c_1 = \cos(\theta - \alpha)\), \(c_2 = \cos(\theta + \alpha)\).
port, record
a port is one of the analyzer’s two answers, \(\theta\) or \(\perp\); each port outcome is a record, and the ports are the branches of the disagreeing case.
pair
the agreeing superposition of the two tag-nodes.
straddle
the analyzer angle Bloch-orthogonal to the bisector, the best which-tag question; the bisector itself is the erasing question.
capacity
\(D\), how well the record’s answer lets one guess the tag.
visibility
\(V\), fringe contrast: per-port for the screen sorted by the record, unconditional for the screen ignoring it.

The deduction

  1. The pair, by pruning. Determinate nodes form by the pruning of all paths that do not pass through them. The or-form of that pruning keeps exactly the augmented paths through one slit-and-tag or the other: a single agreeing object whose spacetime face spans both slits and whose Bloch face spans both tag nodes. The fundamental theorem protects it for as long as the which-tag attribute stays hidden in principle.
  2. The record, and where the divide falls. The analyzer ends that hiding by an exact amount: pass-or-block is a record correlated with the tag, so the top-level structure downstream is the disagreeing superposition over the two ports, weighted by Bloch-face numbers alone, \[ p_\theta = \tfrac12\big(c_1^2 + c_2^2\big), \qquad p_\perp = 1 - p_\theta . \] These are the end-analyzer readout probabilities, with no reference to the screen; at \(\alpha = 30^\circ\), \(p_\theta = \tfrac12 + \tfrac14 \cos 2\theta\).
  3. Within each port, the pair persists, tilted. A pass does not resolve which tag, since both tags project onto the port with nonzero amplitude, so inside each port the agreeing pair stands, tilted to \(c_1^2 : c_2^2\), and amplitudes compose: \[ I_\theta(x) \propto c_1^2 + c_2^2 + 2 c_1 c_2 \cos\big(k\,\Delta(x)\big), \] \[ V_\theta = \frac{2\,|c_1 c_2|}{c_1^2 + c_2^2}, \quad P_\theta = \frac{|c_1^2 - c_2^2|}{c_1^2 + c_2^2} . \] The identity \((2c_1c_2)^2 + (c_1^2 - c_2^2)^2 = (c_1^2 + c_2^2)^2\) gives \(V_\theta^2 + P_\theta^2 = 1\) at every analyzer angle.
  4. Across ports, weights add. Ports are record-separated branches, so their intensities add with their weights, never their amplitudes, \[ I(x) = p_\theta\, I_\theta(x) + p_\perp\, I_\perp(x), \] and the two ports’ cross-terms sum to \(2\cos 2\alpha\). The unconditional pattern therefore has visibility \(c\) whatever \(\theta\) is: no-signaling arrives as an identity of the construction.
  5. The resolution ledger. The record’s separating capacity is \(D(\theta) = |\sin 2\theta|\,\sin 2\alpha\): zero at the bisector, where the question erases the tag distinction, and maximal at the straddle, \(D^\star = \sin 2\alpha = \sqrt{1 - c^2}\).

Classical random tagging

Disagreeing, a mixture: weights compose, no kernel.

\[ \begin{gathered} D = p, \quad V = 1 - p \\ \Longrightarrow\; V + D = 1 \end{gathered} \]

The chord.

Coherent pure tagging

Agreeing: amplitudes compose, kernel overlap \(c\).

\[ \begin{gathered} V = c, \quad D^\star = \sqrt{1 - c^2} \\ \Longrightarrow\; V^2 + (D^\star)^2 = 1 \end{gathered} \]

The circle.

3The theorem underneath

The powerset invalidation theorem

The two-tag deduction is one instance of a classification that covers every multi-slit experiment under every detector configuration. It replaces the Heisenberg cut with a partition: coherence survives within a class of slits no record can tell apart, and dies across any two classes some record separates. Nothing else decides it: no size, no mass, no observer.

Definition (detector signature). For an experiment with slits \(s_1, \ldots, s_n\) and detectors \(d_1, \ldots, d_m\), each firing on the traversal of any slit in its designated subset, the signature of a slit is \[ \sigma(s_i) = \big(s_i \in d_1,\; s_i \in d_2,\; \ldots,\; s_i \in d_m\big). \] Two slits are observationally indistinguishable when their signatures agree; the classes of that equivalence, \(E_1, \ldots, E_k\), partition the slits.

Theorem (Powerset Invalidation). Assume the record structure of the declared configuration is complete and persistent: each slit’s signature includes every record of its traversal accessible within the configuration’s declared refinements, and every declared refinement retains a descendant stating each such record. Then, relative to the Aethus \(A\) doing the classifying, every element \(T\) of the powerset \(\mathcal{P}(\{s_1, \ldots, s_n\})\) falls into exactly one of three categories:

Valid. \(T = \varnothing\), or \(T = E_j\) for some \(j\).
Second-postulate invalidation. \(\varnothing \ne T \subsetneq E_j\) for some \(j\): over-specification within a class.
Third-postulate invalidation. \(T \cap E_i \ne \varnothing\) and \(T \cap E_j \ne \varnothing\) for some \(i \ne j\): a combination across classes.

The three categories are mutually exclusive and jointly exhaustive.

Four slits, two detectors

Choose which slits each detector fires on. The signatures, the classes, and the verdict on all sixteen elements of the powerset follow at once.

ABCD

valid invalid, second postulate (P2) or third (P3)

Three things follow. Moving or erasing a detector redraws the partition and re-bins every case, so the double slit, the quantum eraser and delayed choice are one theorem. Feynman’s indistinguishability rule gets a non-circular ground: amplitudes add within a class, probabilities across, and the boundary between the two additions is the partition. And the second and third postulates divide the invalid cases into two complementary regions with no overlap, too narrow within a class and too broad across classes, the only two ways an agreeing superposition can fail.

4The theorem, iterated

The validity feedback principle

A record is any degree of freedom correlated with an alternative and stated in some refinement of some Aethus. Nothing called measurement appears in that definition. So once collapse is read as invalidity, anybody gets a say in it.

The validity feedback principle. The invalidity of the powerset invalidation theorem’s elements can come from any heterogeneous source, including deduction, to a fixed point, from information provided or providable to the Aethus by the quantum system itself.

Formally, with \(R(T; \mathcal{V})\) the records the channels make available against a candidate cluster \(T\) when the family of valid clusters is \(\mathcal{V}\), the valid family is the fixed point \[ \mathcal{V} \;=\; \{\, T : \text{no record in } R(T; \mathcal{V}) \text{ separates the alternatives of } T \,\}, \] and quantum collapse is this fixed point, reached by the channels jointly, and not the act of any one of them.

The channels are heterogeneous in kind:

  • decoherence-mediated information gain, as when the charged leptons’ mass splittings are large enough that production and environment record which lepton was made, while the neutrino splittings lie below any accessible record and the flavor superposition stands agreeing and oscillates;
  • a qualitative detector placed at a slit;
  • a gravitational or spacetime-induced passage of a record;
  • the quantum case’s own verdicts fed back into the quantum case, as in the two-tag configuration, where the analyzer’s port record ends the hiding of the which-tag attribute and the agreeing pair persists inside each port, tilted by that verdict;
  • a whole logical circuit of effects an experimenter could set up over detector signatures.

They act not one at a time but in iterative-triggering combination: a channel’s verdict is itself a record, and a record opens or closes further channels. Providable is the third postulate’s reach: information that some refinement would state counts against a cluster now, the postulate quantifying over the whole refinement future of the Aethus at once.

General relativity took a monolithic concept, gravitational force, and extended it to a whole medium, a geometry that every source both responds to and shapes. Validity feedback does the same to measurement. It operates iteratively on the powerset invalidation theorem, each verdict a record for the theorem’s next application, and the theorem so iterated supplies at once what the standard formalism supplies by two separate stipulations: the measurement, as the invalidation of the agreeing elements a record separates, and the preferred basis, as the signature partition the records induce.

Copenhagen quantum mechanics keeps measurement as a conceptual monolith, marked by hand and qualitatively, and indexes measurements by a sequence that runs forward in proper time only, the Hamiltonian evolving the state between them. Here they are indexed by determinate nodes, with the path integral weighting what passes between nodes, and a verdict can land, across proper time, on an alternative held open since an earlier node.

The grade is stated with the principle. That validity closes at a fixed point is the postulates’ own demand; which physical processes are channels, and at what rate they pass records, is the physics’ to supply.

5The modal face

Counterfactuals by reopening

The machinery validity feedback runs in physics is first a semantics for counterfactuals, and it needs no similarity ordering over worlds. To evaluate if \(X\) had been the case, \(Y\) would have been at an information state \(A\):

  1. Reopen. Blank the antecedent’s attributes, clearing what they are declared to rest on, while keeping the content the chosen scope protects. The intermediates are the largest restrictions of \(A\) that do this.
  2. Restate. Assert the antecedent in each intermediate.
  3. Prune. Keep the maximal refinements consistent with the standing laws, which are declarations of joint invalidity and are never retracted. These are the pockets of the residue.
  4. Read the residue. \(X \mathrel{\Box\!\!\rightarrow} Y\) when every visible pocket affirms \(Y\); \(X \mathrel{\Diamond\!\!\rightarrow} Y\) when some does; with a weight kernel, the probability of \(Y\) under \(X\) is \(Y\)’s share of the residue’s weight. Would, might and the probability that are one residue read at three grains.

Theorem (non-preferentiality, on the dual-pin reading). Under the dual-pin reading of conjunctive antecedents, there is a finite, fully supported model with a four-attribute state \(A\) on which the would-relation \(\mid\sim_A\) violates Cautious Monotony, and Rational Monotony on the same instance. Consequently no preferential consequence relation in the sense of Kraus, Lehmann and Magidor extends \(\mid\sim_A\), and in particular none induced by Lewis’s or Stalnaker’s semantics.

The witness

Four two-valued attributes \(\mathsf{X}, \mathsf{S}, \mathsf{Y}, \mathsf{Z}\). The laws declare \(\{s, \neg y\}\) invalid, so \(s \rightarrow y\), and \(\{x, s, \neg z\}\) invalid. \(\mathsf{S}\) is the sole declared ground of \(\mathsf{Y}\). The state \(A\) states \(s\), and closure adds \(y\); \(\mathsf{X}\) and \(\mathsf{Z}\) are blank.

AntecedentIntermediatePockets of the residue
\(x\)\(\{s, y\}\)\((x, s, y, z)\)
\(x \wedge y\), dual pin\(\varnothing\)\((x, s, y, z)\), \((x, \neg s, y, z)\), \((x, \neg s, y, \neg z)\)

Under \(x\) the one pocket carries \(y\) and \(z\), so \(x \mid\sim_A y\) and \(x \mid\sim_A z\). Under \(x \wedge y\), reopening \(\mathsf{Y}\) clears its only ground \(s\), and restating \(y\) does not bring \(s\) back; the pocket \((x, \neg s, y, \neg z)\) fails \(z\), so \(x \wedge y \not\mid\sim_A z\), and Cautious Monotony fails at \((x, y, z)\).

The representation results of the ordering tradition characterize preferential relations, and this relation lies outside that family, so the absence of a world-similarity ordering is a fact about the relation and not a choice of notation. The result is a result about the dual-pin reading, an interpretive commitment stated as one: under the Boolean reading nothing is cleared and Cautious Monotony holds on the witness, and which reading ordinary-language counterfactuals follow is an empirical question.

The physical face runs the same four moves, reopen, restate, prune and read the residue, with one addition the modal face does not need: irreducible agreeing superpositions are sometimes admitted as primitive states.

6The axioms

The Aethic tree: axioms and algebra

Everything above runs on four statements and one composition. They are listed first, in the wording the long paper fixes, and then unpacked as algebra.

First postulate of the Aethus

Any attribution of reality is a statement of relation to a particular Aethus.

Second postulate of the Aethus

If an Aethus is valid, then any given attribute will be in Aethic superposition relative to it if and only if it is nonpresent to it.

The Aethic union principle

Jointness is primitive and exclusion is an achievement. A classical or is a jointness whose alternatives carry declared mutual exclusions; a superposition is a jointness whose alternatives do not; and the difference between them is written in the alternatives, not in the logic that composes them.

Third postulate of the Aethus

If some Aethus is valid, then there exists a proper child Aethus to it for which every one of its own proper child Aethae are valid.

\[ \mathbf{V}[A] \implies \exists B \le A,\ \forall C \le B,\ \mathbf{V}[C] \]

Unpacked

States
An Aethus is a deductively closed partial assignment of values to attributes. What it leaves unanswered is a blank, and by the second postulate a blank is an agreeing superposition over the attribute’s answer space, constructive rather than epistemic, and tense-free: an unlearned past is exactly as open as an unformed future.
Joint assertion
The product \(A \cdot B\), the Aethic intersection, has content \(\mathrm{Cl}(\mathrm{C}(A) \cup \mathrm{C}(B))\). A child of \(A\) is \(A \cdot C\) for some \(C\). The product of two distinct determinate states of one attribute is the empty filling \([X : \varnothing]\), an Aethic contradiction, and it stands as a tag: an invalid Aethus keeps its content, and nothing follows from it beyond what it states.
Union and addition
The Aethic union \(A \cup B\) is the last common ancestor, with content \(\mathrm{C}(A) \cap \mathrm{C}(B)\). The Aethic addition \(A + B\) keeps both operands as terms. With \(C = A \cup B\), \(A = C \cdot C_A\) and \(B = C \cdot C_B\), \[ A + B = C \cdot (C_A + C_B), \qquad A \cup B = C, \] so the addition retains what each adds beyond the shared parent and the union forgets it. The unweighted universe \((U, \cup, \cdot)\) is a commutative semiring under an explicit distributivity hypothesis, which holds where a carving separates the terms and fails at irreducible superpositions.
Weights
The weighted Aethae are the monoid semiring \(T = M[U]\): finite weighted sums over \(U\), multiplied bilinearly. The factorization \(aA + bB = C \cdot (aC_A + bC_B)\) is derived from the multiplication, not imposed beside it, and every weighted sum admits the factored form. Weights live in a semiring without additive inverses, so nothing cancels a valid contribution.
Validity
The invalid Aethae form a monoid ideal \(I\) of \((U, \cdot)\), and the sums supported on them, \(M[I]\), form a semiring ideal of \(T\). The validity quotient sends every invalid term to zero and leaves every valid one untouched: it is the contracted monoid semiring, and probability is valued there. Explosion holds on the quotient and not before it: for invalid \(Z\) and any \(A\), \(ZA\) is invalid, so validity-equivalent to \(Z\), and \(A\) is a parent of \(ZA\).
The third postulate as a fixed point
Proper children are the visible terms of a parent’s declared decompositions, and the tree is ordered by them. Validity is a two-generation safety property on that order, and where invalidity is generated by the base of contradictions it is exactly the greatest fixed point \(\nu F\) of the safety operator over that base; the doomed Aethae are the least fixed point \(\mu G\) of the dual operator, its complement. Because the order is transitive, both are reached at the first stage, and in a model with finitely many attributes the doomed Aethae are exactly the tagged ones. A superposition every declared refinement keeps a contradicting record for is invalid now: collapse is checkmate, not an event.
