Welcome to aethus.org

The research of Ajax Benander. Developing Aethic reasoning: a graph-theoretic framework for quantum foundations, the ontology of time, and the measurement problem.

A framework proposing that wavefunction collapse is not a physical process but a logical necessity — the universe enforcing its own consistency. Built on three postulates, it derives the double-slit result, resolves EPR without violating locality, and makes experimentally testable predictions that differ from standard quantum mechanics.
