What kind of claim this is

A unification, priced honestly.

The claim

Compression, not a rival to QM's numbers

The framework reproduces standard quantum phenomenology in every case it treats — the double slit, Bell/EPR correlations, delayed choice, Wigner's friend — and shows that several problems long treated as separate are one problem, solvable once. In the spirit of relativity compressing gravity into geometry.

Its two structural headlines survive every attempt to compress it: the similarity ordering is deleted, not refined, and the framework buys counterfactual substance without counterfactual definiteness — exactly the combination Bell-type theorems demand and hidden-variable programs cannot supply.

The status

Independent, unreviewed, open

This is independent research with public preprints and step-by-step derivations. It has not yet been peer-reviewed; no physicist or philosopher has published a response. The papers are open — the honest thing is provisional interest paired with the caution that the absence of review calls for.

What the framework offers in place of authority is a posted price list: every load-bearing assumption is stated, and the places where its commitments outrun its proofs are named rather than hidden. The Questions & Context page takes the hardest objections head-on.

How it is tested

Where the framework meets a lab.

Aethic reasoning shares every standard prediction it treats, so it is not tested by a single decisive tabletop experiment. Its empirical exposure is a portfolio, and its sharpest fork runs against the spontaneous-collapse programs.

Objective-collapse models — GRW, continuous spontaneous localization, the gravitational variants of Diósi and Penrose — predict a scale-induced breakdown of superposition that no quality of information-erasure can prevent. Aethic reasoning predicts the opposite: no breakdown ever, given genuinely permanent inaccessibility of the distinguishing information, with all apparent scale-dependence exhausted by informational accessibility.

The frontier where those predictions diverge today is real and active: matter-wave interferometry of ever-more-massive systems and its levitated-optomechanics descendants. Each confirmed extension of coherence to larger masses under controlled erasure is evidence for the informational boundary and against the collapse family; a reproducible breakdown not attributable to residual environmental records would falsify the framework outright.

The idealized endpoint of that program — a fully hidden macroscopic scenario that interacts with its own alternatives, with no wave pattern — is the framework's Class C regime. Its realizability is an explicitly open engineering-and-theory question (the exact conditions of in-principle irretrievability), flagged in the papers rather than papered over. Nearer to hand, a two-tag interferometry setup prices the framework's central distinction — between the coherent "agreeing" regime and the classical "disagreeing" one — directly on the wall, and the framework's non-negative weight algebra is a standing structural commitment at the negativity–contextuality joint, not a preference.

The companion framework

Nexic reasoning: the same logic, aimed at deep time.

If reality is indexed to what an observer can know, there is no principled line between the physical constants anthropic reasoning already studies and the contingent historical events that produced us. Both are requisites for your existence; both are anthropic parameters.

The Accordance Principle is the engine: applied one attribute at a time, it says to expect each feature of your natural history to be the likelier route to observers — a deductive generalization of survivorship bias, run as an optimization query over the fossil record rather than a wait for the next dig. The Theorem of Aimless Optimation supplies a built-in control: for any history statistically independent of observers, the baseline and the survivorship-weighted expectation must agree. Sister clades are the natural test — Old World monkeys diverged from our lineage ~25 million years ago and, across 100-plus species since, not one reached ape-level intelligence.

Cascaded across the independent "Miracles" our existence required — the K–Pg extinction, the Chicxulub impactor's specific mass, a single gene loss in our primate ancestors — the per-biosphere probability of observers compresses to something near 10⁻²⁷ per planet. On that arithmetic the Fermi silence is not a paradox but a prediction, and the Rare Earth hypothesis is derived rather than assumed.

Provenance, stated plainly because it can be checked. The framework's fundamentals and its extrusion principle date to August 2022; the three postulates, the invalidation argument, and the re-indexing semantics were published in May 2023 — three years before any AI involvement — and the complete formalism was deposited at PhilArchive in November 2024. Successive deposits (July 2025, January 2026, April 2026) date the later-coined terms — the reduced form, the Quantum Nexus, the Powerset Invalidation Theorem — to the author ahead of any exchange. The 2026 AI assistance was confined to formalization, algorithmic streamlining of the author's own deductions, prose drafting under direction, and literature interface — credited in the papers at the point of use and itemized in the provenance record. The Comprehensive paper has quietly passed 3,600 downloads on PhilArchive in its first eighteen months, currently running above 500 a month, without author promotion.
