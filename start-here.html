Aethic Reasoning

A Framework for Realist Quantum Foundations and Counterfactual SemanticsA proposed realist answer to the quantum measurement problem — reasoning from what an observer can know, not from what the universe is assumed to be.

The one idea

Almost every framework in physics and metaphysics shares a hidden bookkeeping convention: it indexes facts to spacetime. A fact, once located somewhere in the block universe, is determinate full stop; influence travels along worldlines from past to future; alternatives, if they exist at all, live elsewhere — other worlds, other branches, other timelines.

Aethic reasoning makes exactly one move. It relocates the index of reality from spacetime to an observer's information state, called the Aethus. Determinacy stops being a property an attribute has simpliciter and becomes a relation between an attribute and an Aethus. The four-dimensional block universe survives untouched; what varies is not which world exists but which of that one block's co-present decompositions is yours — indexed by your Aethus, selected by the acquisition of information rather than by motion through time. A word to head off the obvious question, because the framework's realism is specific: this is neither a branching multiverse nor idealism. There is one underlying physical block — one spacetime — and the alternative “block universes” are co-present pockets within it — where a pocket is a definite algebraic object, not a spatial or possible-worlds metaphor (defined precisely under the Union Principle below): a maximal internally-consistent attribute-assignment summed into the one block. They are held apart not by any external wall but by each pocket internally negating the others. The Aethus indexes which pocket is yours the way a reference frame coordinatizes spacetime; it does not conjure it. Gaining information is reselection among physically pre-existing pockets — not the proliferation of new worlds (contra Everett), and not the mind-dependent creation of facts (contra idealism). The framework's name for this is plurality without duplication. What an observer experiences as the passage of time is transit along a chain of such observer-indexed block universes, driven by new information; what they experience as collapse is a transition between block universes, never an event within one.

This sounds like a slogan, so let me show immediately that it is precise rather than vague, because that precision is the whole claim. The framework does three concrete things with the inversion, and the rest is consequence:

  1. It supplies a formal semantics for counterfactuals in which the Lewis–Stalnaker similarity ordering is not refined but deleted, and replaced by two quantities the framework already owns — validity and weight.
  2. It reconstructs wavefunction collapse — not as a physical mechanism appended to the Schrödinger equation, but as a greatest-fixed-point pruning condition on a graph of information states.
  3. It identifies a single operation (the “reduced form”) that, run under different weighting regimes, produces quantum entanglement, ordinary Bayesian updating, and the resolution of delayed-choice and Wigner's-friend paradoxes as the same structural phenomenon.

I will take these in turn, with one worked example carried to a payoff, and I will be explicit about what is derived versus assumed, because a pitch that overclaims is one a skeptical reader is right to discard.

What an Aethus is, minimally

An Aethus is not a mind, a belief set, or a “point of view.” It is a mathematical object: the closure under deduction of a set of stated-attributes — resolved answers equipped with the machinery to say which combinations are jointly realizable. Crucially the basic object is the attribute — a question-shaped thing with a fixed set of possible values — not the event (an occurrence in spacetime). “Which slit did it take?” is an attribute with values {1, 2}; “spin along z?” has values {up, down}; “did Caesar cross the Rubicon?” has values {yes, no}. A stated-attribute is one of these questions with its value resolved; a blank is the same question left open. Events survive as the special case of attributes projected into spacetime, but the attribute idiom also handles identities, dispositions, historical unknowns, and counterfactual structure within one calculus. This is load-bearing rather than aesthetic: equivalence classes drawn “by feel” shift under redescription, whereas a coarse-graining of attributes is a genuine quotient that inherits its validity facts from the postulates.

The framework runs on three postulates. The first postulate is the relocation itself: every attribution of reality is a statement of relation to a particular Aethus — so “the tree is five meters tall” is not a free-standing fact simpliciter but a determinate attribution relative to an informational perspective. Since the entire framework is an elaboration of this one principle, I state it once and focus below on the second and third postulates, which do the downstream work — and inventory honesty matters:

  • Second postulate: if an Aethus is valid, an attribute is in superposition relative to it if and only if it is absent (blank) from it. Ignorance is not epistemic veil over a determinate fact; blankness is superposition.
  • Third postulate: the collapse condition, derived below.

A note on tense the reader should keep in mind throughout: blankness carries no tense. An attribute can be unresolved regardless of where it sits in proper time. This is why, relative to your Aethus of thirty seconds ago, your friend's parked car remains in disagreeing superposition even though right now you are staring at a determinate one — and symmetrically why a sinkhole's entire underground history can sit in superposition until the moment you learn of it, “collapsing” backward into determinacy across the block. The past you have not yet learned is as open as the future you have not yet formed — “open” in the informational sense, not the metaphysical one: the block is complete, and what is unsettled is only which block is yours. That symmetry is not a quirky add-on; it falls directly out of indexing reality to information rather than to a moving present.

Deleting the similarity ordering

Start with the counterfactual problem, because it is the cleanest demonstration that this is a real framework and not a posture, and because it consumes no physics. If you want a single result to stress-test before granting anything else, make it this one: it is the framework's most self-contained claim, it leans on none of the still-open machinery, and if the deletion of the similarity ordering fails here, the program is in doubt regardless of what the physics chapters promise. The rest of the document is best read as: the same pruning that does this work, run under other weights, is what produces collapse, the powerset classification, and the reduced-form unification.

The reigning theory of “had A been the case, B would have held” is the possible-world semantics of Stalnaker and Lewis: the counterfactual is true iff B holds at the closest A-worlds under a similarity ordering over worlds. This has been enormously productive. Its acknowledged soft spot is the ordering itself. Lewis did define it — explicitly, with priority weights on respects of similarity; the elevator-pitch charge that “closest was never defined” is a strawman and I want to avoid it. The precise charge is that the ordering has never been non-arbitrarily specified: fifty years of literature has probed it with a standard battery (failures of antecedent strengthening, transitivity, contraposition; the would/might distinction; tie cases like Bizet–Verdi; backtracking ambiguity) and refined, restricted, and contextualized it, but never derived it from anything deeper. It remains a free parameter dressed as an analysis.

Aethic reasoning deletes it. The “worlds” relocate from elsewhere into co-present pockets of the one block universe; closeness becomes validity-plus-weight; and Lewis's “small miracle” becomes an auditable operation called perturbation. To evaluate a counterfactual whose antecedent is already settled the other way, you ascend the genealogy of your information state to the nearest ancestor Aethus in which the antecedent attribute was still blank, restate it to the counterfactual value, hold an explicit informational boundary fixed (which attributes you refuse to reopen), and re-run the pruning postulates over the result. The surviving weighted structure is the residue. The clauses are then read directly off it:

  • Would: true iff B holds on every non-negligible-weight pocket of the residue.
  • Might: true iff B holds on some non-null pocket.
  • Grade: the value is the Aethic weight of B over the residue — a genuinely graded, chancy counterfactual, first-class rather than an afterthought.

The claim to your attention, here as everywhere in the framework, is compression: what this buys is that the standard battery comes out structurally rather than by patch. Antecedent strengthening fails because a strengthened antecedent is a different perturbation generating a different residue, so no monotonicity ever gets off the ground. The Bizet–Verdi tie (“had they been compatriots…”) lands as a theorem about superposition structure: both would-counterfactuals fail while both might-counterfactuals hold, and that asymmetry is the resolution Lewis's tie-breaking machinery struggled to motivate. Backtracking readings are not outlawed and not mishandled — they are alternative choices of the held-fixed boundary, made explicit and movable instead of hidden inside a metric. The honest inventory: the semantics adds exactly one modal primitive, the perturbation operator. Rounding that to zero would be a refutable boast; the theory is stronger admitting it.

And here is the bridge to physics, stated carefully because it is the part a physicist will test hardest: a perturbed attribute can land in a residue whose pocket structure is an agreeing superposition — multiple values genuinely co-present and interacting. In that case there is no single definite value the attribute “would have had.” That is exactly the premise Bell-type arguments require — counterfactual definiteness — and it fails natively here. Yet the discourse stays fully truth-apt: the would-counterfactual is false (not gap-valued), the mights are true, the grade is defined. Counterfactual substance without counterfactual definiteness. The literature coordinates are Peres (“unperformed experiments have no results”) and Fine's theorem (CHSH satisfiability is equivalent to the existence of a joint distribution over the four counterfactual outcomes) — and within the framework this receives a direct Aethic correlate: a Bell violation obtains precisely when the Aethic intersection of the relevant counterfactual stated-attributes is an invalid Aethus.

Why two pockets in one place do not see each other: the Union Principle

Before collapse, I owe the reader an account of why, if both outcomes are physically here, you only see one. This is the Union Principle, and it is the framework's most genuinely alien claim, so I will state it plainly. Classical ontology assumes alternatives are kept apart by sitting in different places or different worlds; the Union Principle proposes the reverse — alternatives coexist in the same physical space, and their apparent mutual exclusivity arises from the internal structure of each alternative rather than from any external separation between them.

The proposal is that physical “or” does not exist. Every logical operation — disjunction and conjunction alike — manifests as physical co-occupation of the block universe (“physical AND”). What looks like classical mutual exclusivity is not a physical primitive; it is a matter of algebraic nesting. A disagreeing superposition places its alternatives in the same physical space but has each pocket internally negate the others ($A_i \cap \bigcap_{j\neq i}\neg A_j$). The non-interactivity that makes the road sign appear on only one side comes from the interior of each pocket, not from any exterior wall between them. Remove those interior negations and you get an agreeing superposition: the alternatives now interact — quantum interference is what that interaction looks like empirically.

The claim compresses into a single table — the framework's canonical statement of the principle:

Logical ORPhysical ANDAgreeing$\bigcup_i A_i$

Logical AND (classical)Physical AND—$\bigcap_i A_i$
Classical “OR”Physical ANDDisagreeing$\bigcup_i\left(A_i \cap \bigcap_{j\neq i}\neg A_j\right)$

Read down the Physical column: one entry, four times over — AND. There is no row whose physical manifestation is OR. That is the entire content of the principle. Logical structure is one thing (the Logical and Mathematical form columns, where OR and AND genuinely differ); physical presence is another (co-occupation of the block, always “AND”). What classical physics calls mutual exclusivity is not a physical primitive but the bottom row's shape — a union of pockets each of which internally negates the rest.

It is worth being exact about what a “pocket” is here, since it is load-bearing and not a figure of speech. Formally, a pocket is one term $A_i \cap \bigcap_{j\neq i}\neg A_j$ of the block's disagreeing-superposition decomposition: a maximal internally-consistent assignment of stated-attributes that asserts one outcome and negates the disjoint alternatives. Its index is in general a pseudo-Aethus — a pair $\langle$template, Aethus$\rangle$ recording which attributes are held in agreeing superposition rather than pruned — and a full block universe is the special case whose index is a genuine Aethus (closed under deduction, and the only kind an observer can actually occupy; the pseudo-Aethus is a construction device that always compiles back into one). These terms sum, under Aethic union, to reconstitute the parent block. So the answer to co-present as what? is definite: co-present as summands of one block's algebraic decomposition, indexed into the same spacetime manifold — not as decoherent branches, not as regions sitting elsewhere, and not as Lewisian worlds. They occupy the same physical space precisely because the outer sum realizes as physical co-presence, and they fail to interact only because each interior negates the others.

And the principle is not introduced to manufacture interference; it is forced — and the same stroke that forces it relieves the outer “or” of a double duty it was never suited for. Ask what a universe must look like if it is to be both logically realist — preserving non-contradiction, honoring the post-Jevons inclusive disjunction — and able to hold co-present alternatives inside one spacetime rather than farm them out to separate worlds. The non-interactivity we observe between alternatives has to be seated somewhere. In the classical picture the exterior “or” carries it, which forces that “or” into one-or-the-other choosiness — precisely the exclusive disjunction Jevons's reform discredited, and we have too much faith in inclusive sentential logic to accept a physical “or” that violates it. The only remaining seat is the interior of each pocket (the $\neg A_j$ terms). Move the non-interaction there and two things happen in one motion: mutual exclusivity is explained without any external wall, and the outer disjunction, relieved of the enforcement job, keeps only the job of joining and locating the pockets — so it can at last be a genuine inclusive “or,” coalescent rather than exclusive. That relocation is the Union Principle — reached by elimination, not by decree.

The reason to take this seriously rather than as wordplay: it is precisely the reform Jevons made to Boole's logic, transplanted to physical ontology. Boole's own “+” was exclusive (defined only for disjoint classes); Jevons's inclusive disjunction, with $X+X=X$, is the version the world now (mis)calls “Boolean,” and Boole rejected it in correspondence rather than accept it. The Union Principle claims the physical timeline ontology never underwent the analogous reform — it still treats combinatory operations as choosy rather than coalescent. Bell violations then become not an embarrassment but an expected feature of any world instantiating agreeing-superposition structure — an empirical signature that no assignment of single hidden values could reproduce.

Deriving collapse: the third postulate as a fixed point

Here is the part that most distinguishes this from interpretive overlays. Collapse is derived — in the precise sense pinned down below.

The setup (the “Oliver's soccer field” thought experiment) macroscopic-izes the double slit: people through two doors, an organizer who must see particle-like clustering, never interference. Oliver, blindfolded, runs the experiment; if he removes the blindfold and sees an interference pattern, he can walk over and ask any friend which door they used, and their answer produces an Aethic contradiction with the interference pattern. The puzzle, sharpened, is an inheritance paradox. If a contradiction in a future information state could simply be backtracked to invalidate a present one, then — because the framework grants no privilege to “the same observer later” over any other Aethus — contradiction could pass between arbitrary unrelated Aethae, which collapses the whole relational structure back into a single objective reality. So contradictions may not pass arbitrarily backward. Yet the macroscopic interference state must somehow be invalid from the start.

The resolution is a two-generational condition with a chess reading. An Aethus is in “check” if a contradiction is possible but escapable (some child information-state avoids it). It is in “checkmate” if every child still leaves a contradiction accessible. Checkmate is invalidity. The key recognition is that the threat is self-similar: every version of Oliver who has not yet asked the question is structurally identical, as an Oliver's Aethus, to the original — and the ∀–∃ quantifier alternation of the postulate captures this self-similarity exactly. Formally:

$$\mathbf{V}[A] \implies \exists B \subset A,\ \forall C \subset B,\ \mathbf{V}[C]$$

“A valid Aethus must have some child all of whose children are valid” — equivalently, it should not be impossible to make a contradiction impossible. The macroscopic interference state is invalid because at every step the which-path information remains retrievable in principle (ask, or read entropic traces), so there is no escape — checkmate — while the classical clustering pattern has a clean future and survives.

What lifts this above storytelling is that the recursive structure has a standard mathematical form. Define the doomed set of Aethae as the least fixed point

$$D = \mu X.\big(\mathrm{Base}\cup\{A : \forall B\subset A,\ \exists C\subset B,\ C\in X\}\big),$$

where Base collects the direct contradictions and second-postulate violations. Validity is the complement of $D$ — the greatest fixed point of the dual safety operator. This is exactly the shape of a safety property in the modal μ-calculus, or of the set of non-losing positions in a two-player game against nature, which makes the chess idiom literal: checkmate is membership in $D$. By Knaster–Tarski the fixed points exist for arbitrary branching, so nothing presupposes finiteness. For finitely-branching trees, König's lemma collapses the closure ordinal to at most ω, and the one-step ∀–∃ statement coincides with the fixed-point version; for infinitely-branching cases the ordinal may exceed ω, and the framework holds non-Archimedean weight capacity in reserve for exactly such stratification.

It is worth being exact about what this does and does not derive, because a logician's first reaction will be that the fixed point merely re-dresses the third postulate in μ-calculus notation. The third postulate on its own supplies a local validity condition — a one-step constraint relating an Aethus to its immediate descendants. The fixed-point construction is not a second axiom stacked on top; it is the minimal closure of that local condition under arbitrary chains of informational descent, forced by two facts the framework already owns: invalidity propagates upward (an invalid Aethus is never a centric-unfolding destination, so a blocked child blocks its parent), and reachability runs the other way. What is derived is therefore not validity itself — that is posited, locally — but the global collapse structure that iterating the local condition to its greatest fixed point generates. Collapse is the shape of that closure, not a fresh posit.

I will be honest about the status: a full formal equivalence proof between the postulate-as-stated and the greatest-fixed-point formulation under explicit branching conditions is a clean lemma that the framework flags as natural rather than as completed. The architecture is committed; that particular proof is owed. But the structural payoff is already in hand — wavefunction collapse is not a primitive appended to the dynamics; the framework reconstructs it as the observable shadow of this pruning operation. Decoherence is not disputed (environmental coupling is exactly the mechanism that makes distinguishing information accessible-in-principle); the framework's contribution is to derive from a deeper informational principle why accessible information destroys coherence — what the standard account already captures as a dynamical consequence.

The Powerset Invalidation Theorem

To see the pruning as a real theorem rather than a story, take the multi-slit generalization. Give n slits and any configuration of “OR-type” detectors. Define each slit's detector signature (which detectors fire on it) and the induced equivalence classes (slits with identical signatures are permanently indistinguishable). The Powerset Invalidation Theorem states that every subset T of the n slits falls into exactly one of three categories:

  1. Valid: T is empty or equals one full equivalence class.
  2. Second-postulate invalid: T is a nonempty proper subset of a single class (over-specification among indistinguishable slits).
  3. Third-postulate invalid: T spans two or more classes (a combination a detector can later expose as contradictory).

These are mutually exclusive and jointly exhaustive, with an explicit count (valid non-empty cases = number of classes; P2 cases = $\sum_j (2^{|E_j|} - 2)$; P3 the remainder). The theorem's interest is not its difficulty — it is short once the postulates are granted — but its content: the two postulates carve the invalid region into complementary, non-overlapping halves (“too narrow” and “too broad”), and the two limiting cases fall out cleanly. No detectors: one class, only the empty set and the full set valid — pure interference. One detector per slit: every singleton its own class, all multi-slit subsets P3 — pure particle pattern. Every configuration in between fixes the quantum–classical boundary discretely, as a property of the informational coupling between slits and detectors, not of scale. This is the framework's relocation of the quantum–classical boundary from size to informational accessibility, made combinatorially explicit and exact rather than asymptotic — which also, in this finite setting, supplies the preferred basis (the detector-induced partition itself) that einselection dynamics is often charged with being unable to select on its own.

One operation, many phenomena: the reduced form coupling

The reduced form of an Aethus is the largest substructure surviving this pruning — formally, the supremum, by Aethic parentage, of the proper children all of whose children are valid — and it is provably equivalent (at the appropriate “validity” tier — agreement of realizable content, blind to invalid labels) to the original. When pruning eliminates joint configurations of attributes, the survivors exhibit a correlation that no one put in by hand. Call it reduced form coupling. The claim is that this single operation, varying only in the weights the coupling carries, produces phenomena standardly treated as unrelated. Here the framework is at its most compressive, which is exactly the signature of a foundational theory when it succeeds — Maxwell making electricity and magnetism one, Noether making conservation laws symmetry.

Let me carry one example to a payoff, deliberately a classical one, to show the mechanism is not smuggling quantum physics. Take Monty Hall. Begin with the uniform prior as an Aethus: $\frac13 B_1 + \frac13 B_2 + \frac13 B_3$ (car behind door $i$). Intersect with the host's response structure — if the car is behind your chosen Door 1 he opens 2 or 3 with equal weight; otherwise he is forced to the one goat door. Expanding the product gives a linear combination over all joint $(B_i, O_j)$ configurations. Now take the reduced form: configurations that are Aethic contradictions (the host opening the car's door; the car behind two doors) carry zero weight and drop out. After the host opens Door 3, what survives, normalized, is

$$\tfrac13 B_1 O_3 + \tfrac23 B_2 O_3.$$

The familiar two-thirds is not produced by a conditioning formula. It is the residue of crossed-out joint configurations, their weight flowing to the survivors. The “where the car is” and “which door was opened” attributes are now coupled — by the validity-preserving pruning alone, no causal mechanism invoked.

Now swap the weights. Here the framework's quantum program aims to recover entanglement as this same operation under Feynman amplitudes rather than classical probabilities; the conjecture is that Bell correlations arise from reduced-form coupling endowed with amplitude weights. This is the program's aim, not a discharged proof — the amplitude kernel and the Born measure are exactly the inputs flagged as open below — but the compression claim is precise: the coupling is one mechanism, and only the weights change. The coupling's indifference to proper-time ordering dissolves delayed-choice retrocausality — and the reason is a genuine bilayer: information transfer is local, bounded by light cones, while validity coupling is global and ordering-indifferent, so what is called quantum “nonlocality” is the shadow cast when the second layer is mistaken for the first. The coupling is fixed over the block; only the random outcome may be gathered in either temporal order. The same machinery enforces Wigner's-friend and Frauchiger–Renner agreement: any pocket in which two observers' reports would contradict is pruned by the third postulate, leaving only the consistent ones.

The methodological point the framework presses, and I find it the honest one: the reduced form was not engineered to unify these. It was defined for an independent reason (it is just the third postulate stated positively), and the unification fell out afterward — which is mild evidence it tracks real structure rather than post-hoc convenience.

Positioned, not strawmanned: RQM, QBism, EPR, Wood–Spekkens

The framework positions itself carefully, not by dismissal.

Relational Quantum Mechanics (Rovelli). RQM is the closest ancestor and is credited as such: its insight that quantum states are observer-relative dissolves EPR nonlocality by removing the assumption of an observer-independent state rather than adding machinery. Aethic reasoning extends from precisely this. The declared differences are specific: RQM interprets the existing formalism, whereas this framework aims to derive quantum phenomena from an informational ontology prior to the formalism; it supplies a concrete mechanism for perspectival change (the block-universe reselection RQM leaves unspecified); and it addresses the multi-observer consistency problem — RQM's known pressure point, where recent work adds auxiliary axioms — through the third postulate, under which contradictory Aethae are structurally barred from interacting as an algebraic consequence.

QBism. Shared: agent-centrality and the informational character of the state. The parting: QBism is deliberately silent on ontology; this framework is explicitly ontological — informational structure is a feature of reality, not merely of an agent's expectations. It is, in fact, not naively ψ-epistemic (nor ψ-ontic): it has an irreducibly ontic component (agreeing superpositions, which exist independently of your knowledge) tied to an epistemic one by the first postulate — which is exactly why the Pusey–Barrett–Rudolph theorem does not bite, since PBR quantifies over ontic-state backgrounds that the framework's central category refuses.

Wood–Spekkens / Allen et al. on quantum causation showed that no classical causal story (A→B, B→A, or common cause) explains Bell correlations without fine-tuning, and called for an exotic-but-logically-realist notion of causation. The framework explicitly answers Spekkens's stated criterion — offering the reduced form coupling as the realist causal substrate, with a conjecture (clearly labeled as such) that the quantum-Reichenbach factorization condition of Allen et al. is the density-operator image of reduced form coupling across a shared parent Aethus.

The EPR resolution itself. The primary claim is not locality preservation (a corollary) but dissolution of the preferred-foliation problem. Copenhagen needs a privileged spacetime slice to define an objective “first measurement”; relativity forbids one for spacelike-separated events. By making collapse an observer-relative transit between block universes rather than an event on any slice, no privileged foliation is needed. Relative to Alice's prior Aethus, Bob's outcome is in superposition; relative to her subsequent Aethus, it is represented as having been determinate all along — so the prior Aethus is genuinely non-hidden-variables while the subsequent one is effectively a hidden-variables model, and Bell's theorem (which rules out any single local hidden-variables theory) is untouched because the framework offers not one theory but two mutually irreducible descriptions indexed to different Aethae.

What is derived, what is assumed, what is open

Credibility here requires the ledger. The framework also carries a more ambitious object at its quantum wing — the Quantum Nexus, a path-integral expression in which an Aethus-valued factor $A(T)$ multiplies each subset $T$ of paths, so that invalidating an Aethus drops the entire powerset element to zero measure and the pruning acts inside the integral. It is branded an offshoot, not part of Aethic reasoning proper: removing it does not collapse the framework. With that noted, the honest accounting:

Derived from the postulates: collapse as a fixed-point pruning structure (modulo the flagged lemma below — and note that its physical bite, that pruned Aethae do not occur, is supplied by the realization principle, which is assumed rather than derived); decoherence's efficacy; the quantum–classical boundary's location; the counterfactual battery; Bayesian updating as reduced-form coupling under classical weights; the structural multi-observer consistency results — Wigner's-friend and Frauchiger–Renner report-agreement, as the pruning of every pocket in which reports would contradict — together with the ordering-indifference that dissolves delayed-choice retrocausality; the block-universe ontology (independently of the relativity-of-simultaneity route, which then serves as confirmation).

Assumed / axiomatic: the three postulates themselves; the weight–probability equivalence; a realization principle explicitly flagged as a “hypotheses non fingo” bridge — invalidity is a label in the formalism, and the claim that a labeled-invalid Aethus is physically unrealizable is injected, not derived. The weighted structure is built as a commutative semiring, deliberately without additive inverses, so that invalid Aethae keep unique identities (a design choice the framework argues for, not a lapse).

Open: the quantum program proper — the amplitude-weighted instantiation of reduced-form coupling that would quantitatively recover entanglement and Bell correlations (and the fully quantum forms of delayed choice and Frauchiger–Renner) — is only partially in hand, and rests on the items that follow; the Born rule — the framework absorbs the squared-modulus structure into the Quantum Nexus, but the kernel and the measure remain axiomatic input, deferred to a successor program (active reasoning); the greatest-fixed-point equivalence lemma; the “coherent/quadratic” half of the polarizer program. There is no completeness theorem and no decidability result for the modal fragment, and the framework deliberately does not claim one. These are named as frontiers, not papered over.

Why this is worth a skeptic's time

The honest summary is that this is a single structural inversion — index reality to the observer's information state rather than to spacetime — pursued with unusual discipline through modal metaphysics, probability, and quantum foundations, and that several apparently independent results turn out to be the same result in different costume. What it is not is vague: the postulates are stated, the pruning is a greatest fixed point, the reduced form is a supremum, the counterfactual clauses have truth conditions, and the theorem has a proof. Whether it is right would take years of scrutiny — the axiom at the quantum wing must be motivated, the flagged lemmas closed, the conjectures discharged. But the architecture is precise, the central claims are stated at the grain at which they are actually true, and the framework is forthright about exactly where its commitments outrun its proofs.

If it is right, the historical description would not be “a new interpretation of quantum mechanics” but a new language in which a family of foundational problems becomes one fixed-point construction. And if the program fails, it will fail at a named joint — concretely, at whether amplitude-weighted reduced-form coupling can uniquely recover the Born rule and the Bell statistics with no further hidden structure; if it cannot, the quantum wing falls, and with it the claim that these phenomena are one construction. That the failure point is nameable in advance is the minimum a serious framework owes, and the basis on which it asks for a reader's attention rather than their agreement.

It engages a live research program in quantum foundations but has not itself been peer-reviewed, and no physicist or philosopher has published a response. This page tries to make the framework legible enough that you can evaluate it on its own terms. If you'd rather start with the hardest questions, Questions & Context addresses them directly.

For a century, no interpretation of quantum mechanics has derived why observing a system changes its behavior—it has been assumed (Copenhagen), denied (Many-Worlds), or sidestepped. Aethic reasoning proposes that wavefunction collapse is not a physical event at all. It is a logical necessity: the universe refusing to contradict itself, the way a chess game forbids leaving your king in check.

The framework is built to engage a specific diagnosis. A research program in quantum foundations—associated in particular with Robert Spekkens and collaborators at the Perimeter Institute—argues that the quantum state is best read as a state of knowledge rather than a state of reality, that the genuine nonclassicality of quantum theory is not located in interference, and that a true realist interpretation will require an exotic but still realist account of causation and inference, expressed in a mathematical structure deeper than sets and functions. Aethic reasoning is offered as one candidate for exactly that structure.

Its central move is to reason from the epistemic root. The framework's core object, the Aethus, is the totality of informational context available to an observer. Three postulates run over it, sorting which parts of a system mutually cohere and which mutually decohere—and quantum interference falls out as a projection of that deeper sorting, rather than being the thing doing the work.

The papers are public, the derivations are explicit, and the predictions are testable. Read on for the full picture, or jump straight to the guides. (A companion framework, Nexic reasoning, extends the same logic to natural history; it runs as a parallel track, and there is an optional summary of it at the foot of this page.)

The Realist Program

Start with the experiment everything turns on. Fire single particles at two slits and they build an interference pattern, as if each went through both slits at once. Place a detector at the slits and the pattern vanishes—each particle picks one. No energy is added, no force exerted. The only thing that changed is information: whether the path could, in principle, be known.

This is the quantum measurement problem, and the trouble is not that we lack a description of it—it is that every description leaves something unexplained. Copenhagen posits collapse as a brute axiom and draws an arbitrary line between the quantum and the classical. Many-Worlds denies collapse happens at all, at the cost of duplicating reality. None of them derive the transition: none explain, from logical principles alone, why the detector changes the outcome.

One way of saying what has gone wrong is the one developed by Robert Spekkens, whose stated research aim is to unscramble the quantum omelette: E. T. Jaynes's image for a theory that has thoroughly mixed together the part describing reality and the part describing our knowledge of it. On this diagnosis, treating the wavefunction as a literal description of reality is a category mistake. The wavefunction looks far more like a state of incomplete knowledge, a probability distribution over something deeper.

The program, in its own terms The realist program does not ask us to abandon realism; it asks us to relocate where the strangeness lives. Its conjecture is that the classical realist toolkit (physical states as a set, dynamics as functions, knowledge as Bayesian probability) is what has to give. What replaces it should still be recognizably about causation and inference, with correlations still getting genuine causal explanations and inference still proceeding stepwise, but the substrate underneath should be something more exotic than sets and functions, the way relativistic space and time are still space and time but no longer the Newtonian ones. The task is to find that substrate, and to find the principles that pinpoint quantum theory within the space of possible theories, much as Einstein's two postulates pinpoint special relativity.

That is the program Aethic reasoning sets out to answer. It does not arrive at the realist conclusions by importing them; the framework was developed independently, by Ajax Benander between 2022 and 2023, and only later recognized as converging—strikingly closely—with this articulation of what a satisfying realist theory would need. What follows is the framework's proposed structure, and why it claims to fit.

Reasoning From the Epistemic Root: The Aethus

The framework's foundational object is the Aethus: the complete epistemic and informational context available to a given observer, taken as a deductive root. Everything else is deduced from it. This is the proposed answer to the call for a structure deeper than sets and functions—not because an Aethus cannot be written down with sets, but because the level at which it does its work is the relational, informational structure of what is knowable, not numerical states or functions between them.

Put concretely: an Aethus is the block universe defined or located exactly by an agent's full sum of experiences and identity. It kind-of overlaps with what one might informally call mind or soul without being either, but it is squarely characteristic of who the agent is in the moment and of what their grasp on reality consists.

▸A second convergence, from a different starting point—Müller's algorithmic idealism

This picture is significantly convergent with what Markus P. Müller's algorithmic idealism program calls a self-state: a first-person-fundamental object that the agent, in some precise sense, is, with the external world recovered downstream as a structured prediction over what such a self-state should experience next. (Müller is a group leader at IQOQI Vienna and a visiting fellow at the Perimeter Institute.) The two pictures were arrived at independently, and from very different starting points—algorithmic information theory in Müller's case, the quantum measurement problem here—so the convergence is structurally separate from the convergence with Spekkens's program already described.

Where they diverge, in what Ajax flags as the Internal-External Paradox (Principle 41), is that the self-state convention puts the observer in superposition over a pre-existing external background, whereas the Aethic convention places the observer at the inferential center with the outside universe in perturbative superposition around them. That difference is what lets agreeing superpositions be definable as a unified category with disagreeing ones.

The claim that the Aethus is a structure deeper than sets and functions is more specifically a claim about the operability domain rather than the axiomatic-compositional one. An Aethus can be written down in terms of static sets (the framework calls them static Aethae), but the level at which it does its work sits one step further abstracted, operating over the structural compatibility between states in the context of attribute unions. The quantum wavefunction is the useful analogy. The wavefunction operates over abstract Hilbert space and concretely projects to standard Born-rule probabilities; the Aethus does something structurally similar one further level upstream, operating over a more abstract relational layer and concretely projecting to a powerset structure (the powerset invalidation theorem of §6 of the paper is the canonical case). Even the wavefunction is, on this picture, downstream of the Aethic abstraction layer. The three postulates run on that upstream layer; what they look like one level down, at the level of wavefunctions, is a sorting: sub-parts of a system that mutually cohere on one side, sub-parts that mutually decohere on the other, with the wavefunction's empirical behavior following from how that sorting comes out.

Two consequences follow immediately, and they are doing the heavy lifting.

First, ignorance is ontologically productive, and operable. What an observer's Aethus does not contain is not merely absent: a conceptual blank manifests as a physical superposition of every state it could be. Ignorance does not sit there waiting to be resolved; it generates superposition, and that superposition is itself operable, a structure the framework can reason and deduce over directly. This is the framework's Second Postulate. In something like the spirit of Gödel, it treats the permanent unknowability of certain attributes not as a defect to be patched but as a constitutive feature of reality. And rather than stopping there and calling the universe logically unfounded, it climbs one level of abstraction and operates over those unknown states themselves. The model is Newton: discovering that velocity is purely relative did not lead him to conclude there were no laws of motion, but to recognize that the change in velocity is what the laws constrain. The Second Postulate is the counterpart, for epistemology, of what Newton's second law is for motion: it does not legislate what is known versus unknown; it gives structure to the concept of ignorance itself.

Second, time is information gain. The framework replaces a single evolving universe with a Markov chain of static, complete block universes. What we experience as the passage of time is movement along that chain, driven by learning. When you gain information you do not edit your universe; you shift to a different one whose entire history was always consistent with what you just learned. The prior universe, superposition intact, stays exactly as it was. This is the Extrusion Principle. It is strictly necessary rather than optional scaffolding: without the shift-rather-than-overwrite move, an information update would have to overwrite the blank in the existing universe, collapsing the Second Postulate's blanks back into ordinary hidden variables (placeholders for some determinate value the observer happens not to know), and the constructive use of ignorance just argued for would vanish. The same move is also what lets the framework be observer-relative without becoming incoherent: nothing is overwritten, so different observers can carry genuinely different reality-renderings.

Unscrambling Logic: Agreeing and Disagreeing Superpositions

The most weight-bearing idea in the framework is an unscrambling of how logic is mapped onto physical realization—and it is here that the union principle does its work.

Classically, we silently assume that two states separated by a logical "or" must be physically mutually exclusive: one happens, the other does not, and if both somehow persist they must occupy separate branches or separate worlds. Aethic reasoning makes four moves against this. It (1) separates physical and/or from logical and/or, treating them as different categories rather than counterparts; (2) drops "physical or" entirely; (3) makes physical co-occupation, physical AND, the only physical relation, since the block universe simply contains whatever it contains; and (4) reassigns genuine mutual exclusivity to a special case of logical disjunction rather than to disjunction in general.

What this buys is a new logical primitive. There are now two kinds of "or," and they share the same physical space rather than branching apart:

The Aethic Union Principle. Both logical disjunction and conjunction manifest as physical AND, block-universe co-occupation, differing only in their algebraic structure. An agreeing superposition is an elementary union: its states coexist and can interfere. A disagreeing superposition is a nested union over intersections with negations: each state coexists with the explicit negation of all the others, so the states are physically present together yet observationally mutually exclusive. The difference between quantum interference and classical exclusivity is not a difference in physical presence; it is a difference in nesting.

What makes this work concretely is the concept of pockets. Think of each possible outcome as a pocket occupying the same physical space. Both pockets are here—in one shared block universe, not in separate branches or separate worlds. The question is: why don't they interact? Classical metaphysics answers this by making the outer "or" do double duty: it both places the pockets in the scene and isolates them from each other, since mutual exclusivity is the only way it knows to prevent interaction. This forces the disjunction to be exclusionary—which is why we end up with timeline-branching, separate worlds, and the "choppiness" that sentential logic has already outgrown since Boole and Jevons.

The Union Principle severs the two jobs. Placement is left to the outer disjunction, which simply says "both are here." Isolation is relocated entirely to the interior of each pocket: the nesting Ai ∩ ⋂j≠i ¬Aj means each pocket internally declares that the other pocket's contents do not exist. The outer "or" is freed to be a genuine, coalescent disjunction—no branching, no choosiness—because the non-interaction is handled from within.

And here is the payoff: if a pocket is not compelled to enforce those internal negations—if nothing forces it to declare the other's contents nonexistent—then whatever it leaves unforbidden registers as an agreeing superposition, and the states can interact. This is the structural origin of quantum interference: it is what happens when the interior negations are absent. Interference is not a special quantum addition to classical physics; it is what you get by default when the internal isolation structure is not imposed. The classical world of non-interacting alternatives is the special case, produced only when the Third Postulate's checkmate forces the negations into place.

The agreeing superposition is the single load-bearing entity of the framework: a genuinely coherent logical disjunction. It is the abstract precursor that interference projects from: states in agreeing superposition combine before the Born rule is applied (they add as amplitudes, not as classical probabilities). The classical world is then the other branch of the table, the disagreeing superposition, and ordinary "physical or" turns out to be nothing more than a subcategory of it.

What this names for the program The realist program asks for the conceptual innovation that distinguishes quantum from classical structure to be named: pinned to a specific category, not gestured at through interference. The framework's proposal is that the agreeing superposition is that category: a logically well-defined object that is neither a determinate state nor a contradiction, and that is genuinely alien to classical metaphysics. There is no "physical or" anywhere in classical reasoning that is not already a disagreeing superposition. On the framework's reading, the union principle and the table above are a candidate for what Spekkens has called a Quantum Rosetta Stone: the dictionary that lets the quantum and classical descriptions finally be read as the same language.

The Pruning Structure

Naming the primitive is only half of it. The framework still needs a rule that decides, for any given system, which agreeing superpositions are permitted and which are not—and it derives one rather than assuming it.

The rule is the Third Postulate, and it is a logical "checkmate." Borrowing the chess image: an Aethus is in check if there exists an avenue to a contradiction—some future piece of information that, once gained, would conflict with what the state asserts. It is in checkmate if every available move still leaves such an avenue open—if there is no escape route to a contradiction-free future. The Third Postulate says that a checkmated state is invalid, and the universe prunes it. Crucially this is two-generational: not "every future leads to a contradiction," but "every future leaves a contradiction still reachable." That subtlety is what makes the rule a genuine structural constraint rather than a blunt one.

The cleanest place to watch this operate is a multi-slit experiment, and the result is the framework's Powerset Invalidation Theorem. Take n slits and any configuration of detectors. Two slits belong to the same equivalence class if no detector—present or future—can ever tell them apart. Now look at the powerset of all possible sets of traversed slits, each taken as an agreeing superposition. The two postulates sort every single subset, with no overlap and nothing left out:

Valid (✓): the empty set, or a subset that exactly matches one equivalence class. P2-invalid: a subset that is a proper part of one class—it over-specifies, asserting a distinction the Aethus cannot support, which the Second Postulate forbids. P3-invalid: a subset that spans two or more classes—it combines slits a detector can distinguish, so a future child Aethus could always catch it in a contradiction, which the Third Postulate forbids as checkmate.

These three categories are mutually exclusive and jointly exhaustive—"too narrow" and "too broad" turn out to be the only two ways an agreeing superposition can fail, and they exactly partition the invalid region. What survives the pruning is precisely the set of valid cases, which the universe then places into a higher-order superposition. The deep claim is in the direction of explanation: reality is not positively assembled from states that share some property. It is the residue of what survives pruning—and the same pruning, by eliminating jointly contradictory configurations, is what couples one Aethus to another.

Why this answers the program This is the framework's proposed exotic but realist causation and inference. When two distant measurements come out correlated, no superluminal influence is invoked and no signal is sent: the correlation is simply what is left once the validity-preserving pruning has eliminated every jointly contradictory configuration. Inference still runs stepwise through the Aethic structure, and correlations still receive a genuine structural explanation (the two things the program treats as the essence of realism), but the substrate doing the explaining is the lattice of valid and invalid Aethae, not a set of hidden variables evolving by functions. The Powerset Invalidation Theorem also makes the pinpointing concrete: the quantum–classical boundary is not a fixed threshold of energy or scale. It sits wherever the informational coupling between system and detector places it. Add a detector, split an equivalence class, and the boundary moves.
▸One layer deeper—the substrate beneath

The pruning above is the realization of the program at the level of correlations. The framework's deeper move runs one layer beneath that, at the level of the logical substrate itself—and it is the part most likely to land for a reader of the realist program.

It is a two-step maneuver, neither half of which looks like much in isolation. First, narrow: configurations the classical paradigm calls contradictions—"the electron went through Slit A and not through Slit A"—are reclassified by the Union Principle as Aethic superpositions, not contradictions at all. Only configurations of multiple genuinely disjoint stated-attributes co-stated in the same Aethus survive as formal Aethic contradictions. Second, broaden: the narrowed concept is generalized to invalid Aethus, of which formal Aethic contradiction is just one base case among several. Third-postulate pruning, second-postulate over-specification, and recursive descendant failures are all modes of invalidity; the classical contradiction is the recursion's leaf, not its root.

The combined move redefines what contradiction is. It is no longer P ∧ ¬P. It is the structural property of being an invalid Aethus, with multiple modes. The third postulate's recursive formulation (stated precisely in the main paper) becomes statable only because the maneuver has been completed first—and the reduced form coupling, the Powerset Invalidation Theorem, and the agreeing-superposition category all follow from this single substrate.

This is the move the program has described as what we should be looking for: find the assumption nobody articulated; replace the primitive everyone took as given; watch what falls out. In non-Euclidean geometry the move was applied to "point" and "line." Here it is applied to "contradiction." The vantage from which the third postulate then operates is—in Ajax's own phrase—almost disconcertingly unassuming. A small adjustment to a definition. The same structural register as the relativity-of-simultaneity move: in propositional content, modest; in downstream implications, the foundation of an entire reorganized framework.

A separate but converging result names what cannot host the nonclassicality once it has been defined this way. The Determinate Background Paradox observes that agreeing superpositions are undefinable in any metaphysical picture where reality is assumed epistemologically determinate in the background. An agreeing superposition's job is to convert a permanent absence of accessibility into an ontological primitive—but this conversion fails whenever the elemental unit of reality is taken to be a fully-determined object (a timeline, a hidden state, a self-state over a fixed external background), because every such picture forces the universe into the very determinacy the conversion specifically refuses. The result is a formal impossibility argument with named ruled-out classes, not a soft preference. The Extrusion Principle is what resolves the paradox for the Aethic framework specifically, by separating ontological completeness from epistemic determinacy: a block universe can be ontologically complete while leaving certain attributes Aethically blank, which licenses the agreeing superposition as a primitive rather than as a wash from another timeline.

The structural realization of the program therefore runs at three convergent levels. The reduced form coupling realizes exotic-but-realist causation and inference—Bell correlations, retrocausal patterns, Wigner agreement, even classical Bayesian inference and Newcomb correlations, all as one operation under different weights. The contradiction-displacement realizes the logical substrate beneath that operation—making the redefinition of contradiction the methodological move that licenses the entire apparatus. And the Determinate Background Paradox names the formal impossibility result that explains why the alternative metaphysical pictures cannot reach the same place by other routes. The framework is not one structural alternative to classical inference. It is the substrate–correlations–impossibility triple that, together, completes the shape the program has been pointing toward.

What It Produces

Applied to the original two-slit case, the postulates reproduce both results—and, more importantly, derive the difference between them.

Detector off. Which slit the particle took is permanently unknowable. The Second Postulate puts the path attribute into agreeing superposition. The Third Postulate checks for checkmate and finds none—no future observation can ever reveal the path—so the superposition stands, the paths interfere, and you get a wave pattern.

Detector on. The which-slit information is now permanently available—at any later moment the readout could be checked. The Third Postulate finds checkmate: every future leaves a contradiction reachable. The agreeing superposition is pruned; the system drops to a disagreeing superposition of the two paths; you get a particle pattern. The wave-to-particle transition is derived from logical consistency, not posited as a brute fact.

The same machinery, via the Extrusion Principle, extends to the puzzles that usually need their own special pleading. Because reality is rendered observer-relative and nothing is ever overwritten, the framework treats delayed-choice experiments (the pruning coupling is indifferent to the proper-time order in which the coupled facts are revealed), Wigner's friend scenarios (different observers carry genuinely different valid renderings), and EPR correlations (recovered without superluminal influence, as above) as instances of one mechanism rather than three separate mysteries. The papers carry these derivations in full.

Falsifiable Predictions

The framework's predictions are specific enough to be tested:

The billiard ball experiment proposes that if distinguishing information between two macroscopic scenarios is made permanently unknowable, the scenarios should interact as if both occurred—agreeing superposition at everyday scale. A single clean failure would falsify the framework.

The Quantum Nexus integral proposes a unification of the Feynman path integral with Aethic pruning dynamics. If correct, it would connect the three postulates to the Born rule in what would be the most compact axiomatization of quantum theory yet proposed.

What's Still Open

The framework is a work in progress, and the open problems are load-bearing. The Born rule has not yet been derived from first principles: the current work takes it as an additional axiom, with full derivation deferred to forthcoming work on what Ajax calls Active Reasoning. The billiard ball experiment has not been run, and achieving permanent information destruction at macroscopic scale is a hard engineering problem. And the framework has not been peer-reviewed—no physicist or philosopher has yet published a response. Questions & Context walks through these and other honest limits.

Why This Speaks to the Realist Program

If the framework holds, the claim it makes to the realist program is a fairly precise one. It offers the Aethus as the structure deeper than sets and functions; the agreeing superposition as the named, well-defined category in which quantum nonclassicality actually lives; and the validity-preserving pruning of the Third Postulate as a causation-and-inference substrate that is exotic but still genuinely realist—correlations explained, inference stepwise, locality intact. It would mean wavefunction collapse was never a physical mystery at all, only a logical consequence of a universe that refuses to contradict itself, which we lacked the ontological vocabulary to see.

That is a large "if," and the framework is the first to say so. The point of this page is not to settle it but to make the proposal legible enough to be argued with—which, on the program's own preferred methodology of stating assumptions plainly and then testing them to destruction, is exactly the right place for a new idea to start.

The papers are public, the predictions are falsifiable, and the main paper has quietly accumulated 3,600+ downloads on PhilArchive in its first eighteen months without author promotion, currently running above 500 per month. Whether the framework survives contact with the physics and philosophy communities is what the next few years will decide.

Broader Implication: Counterfactuals Without Possible Worlds

One unexpected consequence of the Aethic framework is that it naturally produces a semantics for counterfactuals—statements of the form “Had A occurred, B would have occurred.” This consequence was not an independent design goal, but instead emerged as a byproduct of the ontology itself. If reality is fundamentally a weighted hierarchy of co-present Aethae rather than a branching collection of timelines, then the classical picture of counterfactual reasoning must itself be reconsidered.

For over half a century, the dominant approach in modal metaphysics has interpreted counterfactuals by appealing to possible worlds. To determine whether “Had A happened, B would have happened” is true, one imagines alternative worlds in which A holds, identifies those most similar to the actual world, and asks whether B holds there as well. The central difficulty of this approach has always been the notion of similarity itself: no generally accepted account exists of what makes one possible world “closer” than another.

The Aethic framework proposes a different picture.

Rather than searching among hypothetical worlds, it performs a controlled perturbation of the single structured reality already under consideration. Evaluation proceeds by returning to the nearest ancestor Aethus in which the antecedent attribute had not yet been settled, replacing that attribute with its counterfactual value, propagating the resulting consequences through the existing validity structure, and examining the weighted residue that survives.

On this view, counterfactual reasoning is not travel between worlds but recomputation within one ontology.

This naturally distinguishes three familiar modal notions.

  • Would: the consequent holds throughout every surviving pocket of non-negligible weight.
  • Might: the consequent holds in at least one surviving pocket.
  • Grade: the weighted fraction of the surviving residue satisfying the consequent, yielding a continuous counterfactual value rather than a merely binary one.

Because these evaluations inherit their weights directly from the Aethic structure itself, probability and counterfactuality become two aspects of the same underlying mechanism rather than independent conceptual primitives.

Several long-standing features of counterfactual discourse emerge structurally from this picture. Context dependence follows from the evaluating Aethus itself rather than requiring special conventions. Engineered dependencies arise from explicit informational structure rather than hidden assumptions. Classical tie cases admit genuine weighted superpositions rather than arbitrary tie-breaking. Most strikingly, quantum counterfactuals can remain fully meaningful without assuming that unperformed measurements possessed unique determinate outcomes.

The resulting picture replaces navigation among possible worlds with perturbations of one weighted reality. Possibility is no longer treated as a primitive landscape through which reasoning moves, but as the local geometry of a structured ontology under controlled alteration.

If this program succeeds, the same machinery that underlies the framework's treatment of quantum mechanics simultaneously generates a unified account of probability, counterfactual reasoning, and modality. The proposal therefore extends beyond physics into metaphysics, suggesting that the logic of “what would have happened” is not an independent layer of reality but a natural consequence of the Aethic structure itself.

Readers interested in the formal development—including perturbation operators, weighted residues, graded counterfactuals, and detailed comparisons with the Lewis–Stalnaker tradition and the standard battery of modal counterexamples—may expand the discussion below or consult Section 9.2, A Formal Semantics for Aethic Counterfactuals, in the accompanying manuscript.

The proposal sketched above is more than a rival way of computing counterfactuals. Its ambition, if it succeeds, is to remove one of the central primitives of modern modal semantics—not refine it, not generalize it, not add to it—and to take a different operation as fundamental in its place.

For most of the past century, counterfactuals have been treated as relations among possible worlds. To evaluate “Had A occurred, B would have occurred” is to consider worlds in which A holds, pick those judged closest to actuality, and ask whether B holds there as well. A large literature has grown up around the notion of similarity—what makes one world closer than another—and around an accumulating catalogue of cases designed to test it.

The Aethic proposal does not enter that literature. It changes the question.

The dominant tradition asks:

Which world is closest?

The proposal asks:

Which earlier informational state should be reopened?

These are not competing answers to a shared question. They are different questions, posed on different objects, and they call for different evaluation procedures. The first treats modal reasoning as navigation across an external landscape of worlds; the second treats it as operation upon the internal structure of one.

That distinction is what the proposal turns on. Counterfactual evaluation, on this view, is no longer comparison across independently existing worlds. It is a controlled perturbation of the single reality already under analysis: locally restate an attribute at the ancestor Aethus where it had not yet been settled, let the validity constraints propagate, and read off the weighted residue that survives.

The picture this implies, stated as a denial and an affirmation:

Counterfactuals are not fundamentally relations between worlds.

They are operations performed on one structured ontology.

The similarity ordering is not corrected, refined, or weakened. It is removed as a primitive. Where most efforts in the field spend their energy improving the similarity relation, the proposal argues it should never have been there to begin with.

The fair objection to a move of this kind is whether the similarity primitive has actually been eliminated or only renamed. If decisions about which ancestor to reopen, which informational structure to hold fixed, or how the validity constraints propagate end up requiring judgments equivalent to selecting a closest world, then the proposal has relocated the central difficulty rather than displaced it. The defence offered in the formal development is that these operations are determined by independently motivated structural features of the Aethic ontology, not by free parameters tuned to the cases the proposal is supposed to handle. Whether that defence holds is what the rest of Section 9.2 is for.

There is also a larger claim in the background, and it is worth stating without dressing:

The claim is not merely that counterfactuals work differently.

It is that modality, probability, and quantum mechanics turn on the same underlying structure.

That is the shape of unifying theories generally—a list of apparently independent phenomena, treated by separate machinery and separate primitives, exhibited as several manifestations of one underlying object. The proposal claims that probability, counterfactuals, branching, and quantum indeterminacy are precisely such a list. Probability is the weighting already present on the ontology. Counterfactuals are controlled perturbations of it. Branching is local informational refinement, not proliferation of timelines. Quantum indeterminacy is what the same structure looks like when it is queried at finer-grained Aethae than classical physics admits. Four foundational notions, four operations on one object.

The semantics is not engineered to fit a pre-existing modal tradition. It falls out of an ontology developed for a different reason—to make quantum phenomena look structurally natural rather than exceptional—and only then turns out to carry a modal semantics with it. The semantics is downstream of the ontology, not the other way around.

None of this would matter if the formal development did not hold up. The proposal's significance is conditional on three things at once: that the mathematics is coherent, that it reproduces the inferential range of established modal semantics across the standard catalogue of cases, and that the same structural machinery proves explanatorily fruitful where the framework touches physics. Each is a serious bar. None of the three on its own would suffice, and the framework is the first to acknowledge it.

If those three conditions hold together, what the proposal would leave behind is not a better answer to the old question but a different question. Possibility ceases to be a separate landscape through which modal reasoning travels and becomes the local geometry of one weighted reality under controlled alteration. Counterfactuals are not journeys to neighbouring worlds; they are recomputations within one ontology. Modality is dynamic rather than comparative—an operation performed on structure, not navigation among alternatives.

And on that picture, the connection to quantum mechanics stops being a happy coincidence. The physical theory and the modal semantics become the same architecture seen from two ends: one queried about outcomes, the other about counterfactuals. Whether the architecture survives expert scrutiny is what the next stretch of work has to decide. The form of contribution, if the form holds, is the one already named: the explanatory compression of several independent foundational notions into a single structural object. That is what the proposal is offered as. Nothing less, and nothing more.

Everything above is the Aethic framework—the quantum side. Nexic reasoning is a companion framework that applies the same epistemic, observer-relative logic to a completely different domain: the history of life on Earth. It is a parallel track rather than a prerequisite, which is why it lives here rather than in the main flow—but the two share a spine, since each takes what an observer can know as its deductive root.

Its founding insight is that there is no principled line between the physical constants standard anthropic reasoning already analyzes (the gravitational constant, the fine-structure constant) and the contingent historical events that produced us (the K-Pg extinction, a specific gene loss in our primate ancestors). Both are equally requisites for our existence; both are anthropic parameters. Nexic reasoning is what you get when anthropic reasoning is allowed to range over historical events, not only numbers.

The Accordance Principle is the deductive engine. Applied one attribute at a time, it says you should expect each feature of your natural history to be the likelier one for producing observers rather than its alternative: a continuous, deductive generalization of survivorship bias. It has two faces worth separating. The methodological Accordance Principle is the inference tool you compute with: given an unknown about Earth's past, bet on whichever alternative was more conducive to observers, which turns natural history into something you can run optimization queries on rather than only waiting for fossils. The ontological Accordance Principle is the deeper and stranger claim: that the principle is not merely a rule for reasoning but the very thing that makes the selection effect exist, the "spark" that boots up the mechanism below. On the framework's own account this is its first genuinely Aethic nonclassicality, since it couples a conscious observer to counterfactual history in a way a purely materialist picture has no room for.

Optimation is that mechanism. It is emphatically not a force, a designer, or teleology; it is survivorship bias operating across the entire space of possible histories, amplified until Earth's past looks engineered without being engineered. The name is meant as "optimal-making": the optimum is emergent residue of vast filtering, not a goal. The matching error to avoid, the Optimation Fallacy, is reading this as "evolution tends toward humans." It does not; we see an apparently observer-directed history for the same reason a lottery winner holds a winning ticket.

How optimal is our history, really? The answer runs through Imperfection Magnitude Drops: alternative histories whose probability of producing observers is drastically, combinatorially smaller than ours. The IMD Nameability Principle is the sharp point: because perturbing any attribute opens far more ways to make a history worse than to keep it near-optimal, the describable alternatives are overwhelmingly concentrated on these drops. Any alternative history you can actually name is almost surely far worse than ours; the near-optimal neighbourhood of our own history is, in the framework's phrase, inarticulable from outside but possessed in full from inside. This is what licenses the Optimal-Earth Conjecture: our history behaves as a local optimum, not globally maximal, not designed, but such that any named perturbation almost surely lowers the probability of observers.

The deepest reframe is the Exotic Principle. Standard Rare Earth reasoning multiplies a list of small, independent probability scalars, Drake-equation style. Nexic reasoning treats that as a lossy projection, much as Born-rule probabilities are a projection of the wavefunction. The real upstream object is exotic structure: an Aethus-valued web of joint, non-independent relationships between anthropic parameters, organic in character, more like the grain of bark than a count of trees. The Exotic Accordance Principle then holds that an established, extraordinarily improbable event in our history is almost surely exotic structure proper, and that exotic structure is almost surely a prerequisite for observers: what the framework formally calls a Miracle. The payoff is methodological: do the inference on the upstream structural layer rather than the flattened scalars, and Nexic reasoning's predictions reach past what flat Rare Earth accounting can support.

One worked example shows the machinery on real data: the sea-level analysis. The Hațeg Island hypothesis below requires that early primates, stranded on a Cretaceous island, later reached the mainland—which needs a substantial drop in sea level in the early Paleocene. The framework derived that requirement before checking, and the sea-level record shows exactly such a decline at roughly 65.95 million years ago. More broadly the study tests the Intrinsic Frequency Principle—the claim that natural history shows certain events recurring far more often than their bare cosmic base rate would allow, which is the empirical fingerprint of Optimation and the kind of evidence the Exotic Principle predicts.

Run to the end, this turns into numbers. Cascading the dinosaurian two-node circumstance and further contingencies, the framework deduces an upper bound near 10⁻²⁷ for conscious life arising on a given planet—roughly one civilization per observable universe. The Rare Earth hypothesis becomes a derivation rather than an assumption.

And it carries a sharp falsifiable prediction. The Hațeg Island hypothesis holds that the earliest primates evolved on Hațeg Island in Late Cretaceous Europe—the one Mesozoic environment known to lack large theropod dinosaurs. A single primate fossil in the Hațeg deposits of Romania would be direct evidence; the early-Paleocene sea-level decline the framework also predicted is already confirmed in the geological record.
