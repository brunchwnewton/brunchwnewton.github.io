Start here · what this work is · a five-minute read

Modal logic,
taken to physics.

This is a research program that takes the tools of modal logic — possibility, counterfactuals, what an agent has records of — and applies them to the foundations of physics. Its object is the information state; its result is a finite-domain representation of quantum measurement in which the quantum–classical boundary is a classification theorem and the measurement update is the representation of a stipulated restriction. Here is what that means, in the order a skeptical reader would want it.

Aethic reasoning determinacy( q ∣ A ) by Ajax Benander modal logic → physics not yet peer-reviewed

What this is, concretely

Modal logic studies what follows from what an agent knows, what could have been otherwise, and how a description is refined as records arrive. Physics has mostly kept that machinery at arm’s length. This program does the opposite: it treats an observer’s information state — the bookkeeping of which attributes have records and which are still open — as a mathematical object in its own right, writes down the consistency constraints such states must satisfy, and asks what physics looks like when those constraints are taken as primitive. The framework is called Aethic reasoning, after its object, the Aethus. Nothing in it is offered as a new ingredient of nature; it is a different choice of what to take as basic.

The three constraints

The first is indexing: a fact is determinate relative to an information state, never simpliciter — determinacy( q ∣ A ) is a two-place relation. The second reads a blank — an attribute no record fixes — not as an unknown value awaiting lookup but as genuinely open: the whole answer space, held as structure. The third is a consistency-pruning rule: a description is valid only if some declared refinement of it has no descendant that contradicts a record, the quantifiers running over the full structure of declared refinements and not merely over whether a record might become available. That rule has a definite logical shape, an existential quantifier over a universal one. What follows combines these three constraints with further structure, named where it enters: a quantitative kernel for the statistics, a stipulated restriction for the measurement update, and a physical premise for applying the criterion to an observer’s laboratory.

What the constraints do on the standard puzzle

Take the double slit. Relative to an information state with no record of which slit was taken, the two paths are a single open structure — and openness has a fingerprint, interference, because nothing anywhere records the difference between the alternatives. Add a detector, and the third constraint fires: a description asserting coherence across the two paths now contradicts a record that exists, so it is invalid, and what survives is the exclusive alternative — one path or the other, weights adding. Erase the record and the coherent description is valid again. In one line: a blank relative to a state is a superposition relative to that state; a record that the state’s declared refinements cannot escape ends it. Blankness is indexed, and not every superposition is alike: alternatives can compose as mutually exclusive, the disagreeing regime, or as co-present without mutual negation, the agreeing regime, and the two differ in what they carry. The line between quantum and classical behavior is a fact about which records a configuration can produce, and the papers prove it as a classification theorem under one hypothesis, a complete and persistent record structure: every candidate superposition then falls into exactly one of three bins fixed by the partition the records induce. No cut is drawn by size, complexity, or observer.

The same criterion is meant to govern an unread detector and an unread friend’s laboratory, and what must be established differs by case:

The technical result

Add the one quantitative rule quantum mechanics already uses — a phase-weighted sum over alternatives, the path-integral kernel — and, on a fixed finite domain, the three constraints and the kernel yield a Hilbert-space representation of the admissible clusters with the Born statistics: states as vectors, superposition as the union of clusters, record observables as self-adjoint operators by a soundness result, no-signalling as an identity for a fixed record partition, and the projection postulate as the representation of a stipulated record-compatible restriction, realized as Lüders conditioning and not selected by the validity rule alone. The representation does not reach every Hilbert state on any one finite domain. The kernel is posited, as every formalism posits its quadratic law, and the restriction is stipulated; what is posited and what is derived is inventoried in the paper’s first section.

The sentence, earned

A reader who has followed the paragraphs above can now be told the strong version at the grade the papers state it. The measurement problem, in its sharpest statement — Maudlin’s trilemma of completeness, linearity, and definite outcomes — is addressed by denying completeness in a way not on the standard menu: the wavefunction is incomplete not because values are missing from it but because the index and the validity structure are. Given the kernel, the stipulated restriction, and one physical claim about records named below, definite outcomes follow relative to an index while the dynamics stays linear. The physical claim is a hypothesis, not a result; the claim is made after the machinery rather than before it, because it is only worth anything after.

The price, posted

Three things are assumed and one is open. The kernel is assumed — its derivation is the program’s stated next problem. The record-compatible restriction that the measurement update represents is stipulated, not derived from validity. One physical claim is assumed: that a record-bearing system of observer grade cannot be kept from exporting its records, which is where the framework would part from universal unitarity and where a physicist who doubts it should press; establishing it for a whole laboratory is the open question. On the finite domain every statistic treated is the standard one; the one proposed divergence from universal unitarity, for a coherently prepared live-or-dud response device, is stated in the comprehensive treatment as a prediction with its premise. And the work is independent research, not yet peer-reviewed, with public preprints and step-by-step derivations — the appropriate posture toward it is provisional interest with the caution that the absence of review calls for. The papers close with a ledger that states every commitment, and the places where the claims outrun the proofs are named rather than hidden.

The rest of the program

The quantum wing is one branch. The same three constraints yield a counterfactual semantics with no possible worlds and no similarity ordering — and the ordering’s absence is a theorem under the stated reading of conjunctive antecedents: the consequence relation fails Cautious Monotony by an explicit countermodel, so no smooth preferential model represents it. They yield a decision rule, adopted as a policy that indexed determinacy motivates but does not select, that delivers one-boxing in Newcomb’s problem under stated dependence assumptions; a reading of anthropic reasoning in which typicality is relocated to information states; and, at the philosophical root, an account of time on which relativity’s time is pure geometry and the resolution of what is open into what is stated belongs to the information state instead. The provenance is dated and checkable: the pruning rule’s mechanism in a journal entry of August 22, 2022; the three constraints complete on May 8, 2023; the formal apparatus, all of it, built afterward on that unchanged base.
