Start here · what this work is · a five-minute read

Modal logic,
taken to physics.

This is a research program that takes the tools of modal logic — possibility, counterfactuals, what an agent has records of — and applies them to the foundations of physics. Its object is the information state; its result is an axiomatization of quantum mechanics in which measurement is derived rather than posited. Here is what that means, in the order a skeptical reader would want it.

Aethic reasoning determinacy( q ∣ A ) by Ajax Benander modal logic → physics not yet peer-reviewed

What this is, concretely

Modal logic studies what follows from what an agent knows, what could have been otherwise, and how a description is refined as records arrive. Physics has mostly kept that machinery at arm’s length. This program does the opposite: it treats an observer’s information state — the bookkeeping of which attributes have records and which are still open — as a mathematical object in its own right, writes down the consistency constraints such states must satisfy, and asks what physics looks like when those constraints are taken as primitive. The framework is called Aethic reasoning, after its object, the Aethus. Nothing in it is offered as a new ingredient of nature; it is a different choice of what to take as basic.

The three constraints

The first is indexing: a fact is determinate relative to an information state, never simpliciter — determinacy( q ∣ A ) is a two-place relation. The second reads a blank — an attribute no record fixes — not as an unknown value awaiting lookup but as genuinely open: the whole answer space, held as structure. The third is a consistency-pruning rule: a description is valid only if it can be refined without ever running into a contradiction with a record that could exist. That last rule has a definite logical shape, an existential quantifier over a universal one, and the papers show the shape is forced rather than chosen. Everything downstream is these three constraints, applied.

What the constraints do on the standard puzzle

Take the double slit. Relative to an information state with no record of which slit was taken, the two paths are a single open structure — and openness has a fingerprint, interference, because nothing anywhere records the difference between the alternatives. Add a detector, and the third constraint fires: a description asserting coherence across the two paths now contradicts a record that exists, so it is invalid, and what survives is the exclusive alternative — one path or the other, weights adding. Erase the record and the coherent description is valid again. In one line: unknown → superposition; knowable → no superposition. The line between quantum and classical behavior is a fact about which records a configuration can produce, and the papers prove it as a classification theorem: for any arrangement of detectors, every candidate superposition falls into exactly one of three bins fixed by the partition the detectors induce. No cut is drawn by size, complexity, or observer.

The technical result

Add the one quantitative rule quantum mechanics already uses — a phase-weighted sum over alternatives, the path-integral kernel — and the three constraints form an axiom set. The main theorem is a recovery: under a fixed assignment of clusters of alternatives to vectors, that set recovers the Dirac–von Neumann formalism axiom by axiom. States are rays; superposition is the union of clusters; observables are the partitions that records induce; the Born rule is the weight of a record-restricted cluster; the projection postulate is pruning followed by renormalization; composite systems are products of path spaces; evolution is the kernel’s composition law; no-signalling is an algebraic identity. Six of the seven standard axioms arrive as theorems. The seventh, the Born rule, is not posited beside the state space but absorbed into the single weight law, the amplitudes recovered from cluster weights by polarization and never held as objects. What is left out of the set is exactly what constitutes the measurement problem in axiom form: there is no measurement axiom, no projection axiom, and no axiom introducing observables. That is a re-axiomatization, not a reconstruction — the kernel is posited, as every formalism posits the quadratic law — and the papers say so in their first paragraph.

The sentence, earned

A reader who has followed the four paragraphs above can now be told the strong version without it costing anything. The measurement problem, in its sharpest statement — Maudlin’s trilemma of completeness, linearity, and definite outcomes — is resolved by denying completeness in a way not on the standard menu: the wavefunction is incomplete not because values are missing from it but because the index and the validity structure are, and once those are supplied, definite outcomes are a theorem relative to an index while the dynamics stays linear. At the grade the papers state it, the measurement problem is solved as a theorem of the axiom set, given the kernel and one physical claim about records named below. That is the claim. It is made after the machinery rather than before it, because it is only worth anything after.

The price, posted

Three things are assumed and one is open. The kernel is assumed — its derivation is the program’s stated next problem, assigned to a geometric successor program on the argument that the constraints are logical in substance where the kernel is kinematic. One physical claim is assumed: that a record-bearing system of observer grade cannot be kept from exporting its records, which is where the framework would part from universal unitarity and where a physicist who doubts it should press. No new numerical predictions are made; every statistic is the standard one. And the work is independent research, not yet peer-reviewed, with public preprints and step-by-step derivations — the appropriate posture toward it is provisional interest with the caution that the absence of review calls for. The papers close with a ledger that prices every commitment, and the places where the claims outrun the proofs are named rather than hidden.

The rest of the program

The quantum wing is one branch. The same three constraints yield a counterfactual semantics with no possible worlds and no similarity ordering — and the ordering’s absence is a theorem, not a preference: the consequence relation fails Cautious Monotony by an explicit countermodel, so no ordering could represent it. They yield a decision rule that delivers one-boxing in Newcomb’s problem from structure rather than from a contest between rules; a reading of anthropic reasoning in which typicality is relocated to information states; and, at the philosophical root, an account of time on which relativity’s time is pure geometry and the resolution of what is open into what is stated belongs to the information state instead. The provenance is dated and checkable: the pruning rule’s mechanism in a journal entry of August 22, 2022; the three constraints complete on May 8, 2023; the formal apparatus, all of it, built afterward on that unchanged base.
