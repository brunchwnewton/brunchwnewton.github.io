The problem, in three sentences

Quantum mechanics is the most precisely confirmed theory in science, and it describes a world of held-open alternatives — superpositions — that evolve smoothly and never pick. Yet every measurement ever made returns exactly one definite outcome. Where the picking happens, and what counts as a “measurement” — that is the measurement problem; a century on, every interpretation still pays a famous price to answer it.

The move

Physics habitually pins facts to points in space and time. Aethic reasoning pins them to information states instead. Determinacy becomes a two-place relation — determinate( q ∣ A ) — a fact q is settled relative to a body of records A, never absolutely. Picture reality as kept in books: every attribute is either stated in your books or genuinely blank. And a blank is not a secret value waiting to be looked up. It is open — and openness has physical consequences.

Why a blank is not just ignorance

“Surely the value is there and you merely don’t know it” is a testable claim: it requires learning to work by overwrite — destroying the open description and writing a value in its place — and the world refuses. Interference is what open alternatives do; it is the receipt the world prints when a distinction is blank, and erasing the record brings the receipt back. Ignorance about a definite value predicts no such receipt. The blank is real.

“Or,” taken seriously — and collapse as bookkeeping

A superposition is nothing exotic: it is alternatives held together while the attribute distinguishing them stays blank — the word or, taken with full seriousness. A detector states the distinguishing attribute. Once stated, the held-together combination can never again agree with any future record: it is checkmated — invalidated the way an inconsistent position is, not crushed by a mysterious physical process. Collapse is pruning. Determinism lives within an information state; the randomness lives across them.

The theorem that runs the machine

Run that rule systematically — over the powerset of a particle’s paths, every combination of alternatives at once — and each combination lands in exactly one of three bins: valid, too narrow (it asserts a distinction no record could ever support), or too broad (a future record can catch it in a contradiction). Move or erase a detector and the bins re-sort. The quantum–classical boundary is literal re-classification — no size, mass, or conscious observer consulted — and the preferred basis every rival account struggles for falls out free. The theorem is playable, live, in the guide.

What it buys, and what it costs

Bought: the measurement problem dissolved rather than decorated; Monty Hall, Bayesian updating, entanglement, and Wigner’s friend as one pruning operation under different weights; and the block universe kept whole — one objective reality, many legitimate determinacies: reality independent, determinacy indexed. The price is posted, not hidden: one separable weight axiom (the Quantum Nexus) adopted the way Newton adopted gravitation; a standing falsifiable fork against spontaneous-collapse models at the matter-wave frontier; and the work is independent research, not yet peer-reviewed — evaluate the papers on their own terms.
