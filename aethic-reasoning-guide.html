Aethic Reasoning: The Framework at Six Depths

One framework, told five times at increasing resolution — the same claims at every level, with nothing softened, only unpacked. Pick the depth that matches what you already know; each level is self-contained, and Level 6 is the papers themselves.

LEVEL 1Assumes nothing. Leaves you with the one move and why it matters.

The idea, in plain sight

Flip a coin and cover it. Ask yourself what, exactly, is under your hand.

The answer everyone carries — so old it doesn't feel like an answer — is that there's a fact under there, heads or tails, and you just don't know it yet. The world finished the job; your knowledge is lagging behind. On this picture, "what could have happened" lives somewhere else: other ways the whole world might have gone, other timelines, other branches. Reality is a corridor of settled facts, and possibility is the wallpaper of corridors you're not in.

Aethic reasoning makes one move against that picture, and everything else in the framework is the consequence of the move. The move: the alternatives are not somewhere else. The openness of the covered coin is not your ignorance of a finished fact — it is a piece of the structure of the room you are standing in, relative to you. The framework's own aphorism puts it in nine words: the multiverse does not contain the room; the room contains the multiverse.

Here is the same move said carefully. Your situation, at any moment, is an information state — everything settled for you, plus everything genuinely open to you. Call that state your Aethus. The claim is that reality is indexed to that state: what is determinate is determinate relative to an Aethus, and where your Aethus has a blank — an entry with no value written in — the world, relative to you, holds the open alternatives as real structure, all of them, in this room, not in distant copies of it. When you uncover the coin and see heads, nothing anywhere "collapses" or splits. You step into a block universe — a complete, permanent, four-dimensional world — in which the coin was always heads, and the prior state of affairs, the one holding both alternatives open, remains exactly what it was: a different, permanent structure, now simply no longer yours. Learning is re-indexing, not overwriting.

One more picture, because it converts the move from strange to almost familiar. You're driving past a forest. At 2:00 you glance left and see a young tree; at 2:05 you glance again and see an old one. The standard story says the car was parked and one tree grew. This framework says you moved — you were always looking at two different trees. Learning is that: motion along a dimension your speedometer doesn't show, past complete and permanent scenes. The multiverse starts at the edge of your senses, not the edge of the cosmos.

Three things follow immediately, and they're the reasons anyone should care.

First, the notorious weirdness of quantum mechanics stops being an exception and becomes the visible tip of a general fact. In the double-slit experiment, an unwatched particle behaves as if it took every path at once, and watching it changes that. On the standard picture this is scandalous. On the Aethic picture it is the one place in nature where the openness of a blank entry leaves a visible fingerprint — interference — because the particle's path is the rare kind of fact that no one, even in principle, could ever check. Your friend's location right now is also open relative to you, and also, on this view, a real superposition — but clues about your friend are everywhere (footprints, phone records, the memory of a passing seagull, so to speak), so the alternatives can't stay consistently open; the structure prunes itself. The quantum/classical divide isn't about size. It's about whether the information is reachable in principle. The whole experiment in one sentence: unknown → superposition; knowable → no superposition.

Second, the past gets stranger and more solid at the same time. Whatever you have seen is permanent — no take-backs, ever, on this view; that part is more rigid than common sense. But whatever no one could ever check about the past was never settled in the first place — not hidden, open — which is less rigid than common sense. Settledness was never about time. It was about information.

Third, none of this is offered as mysticism, and it comes with ways to be wrong. The framework is built from three explicit postulates, it forbids things (no signaling through any of this, no consciousness magic, no changing what's been recorded), and it stakes claims that data could kill — including one it argues data already kills for the main rival way of reading the same idea. If you take nothing else from this level, take the shape of the move: not a new ingredient added to physics, but an old assumption removed — the assumption that "the way things are" comes stamped on the world rather than indexed to the one holding the question.

The next level tells you the actual rules.

LEVEL 2Assumes general-reader comfort. Leaves you with the three postulates, the two superpositions, and the boundary.

The framework in one sitting

The object everything is indexed to

An Aethus is an information state: a set of attribute-entries, each either stated (a value written in: the coin: heads) or blank (no value: the coin: —). Anything that keeps records has one — you, a lab notebook, a detector, a grain of rock bearing an isotope ratio. Two bookkeeping rules matter. An Aethus is closed under deduction: everything your entries jointly imply is part of the state, whether or not you've noticed. And two Aethae presenting the same total information are the same Aethus — the state is the equivalence class, not any particular list.

One distinction carries the whole construction, and a small example fixes it. Suppose all you know about your friend Tim is that he has a dog named Biscuit. What color is Biscuit? "Brown and not-brown at once" is a contradiction — forbidden, absolutely, everywhere in this framework. But no color entered at all is not a contradiction. It is a blank, a single consistent entry in its own right — formally missing, yet a first-class constituent, not an absence. "Brown and not brown" is forbidden; blank is allowed — and standard set-vocabulary cannot hold that difference, which is precisely why the Aethus exists as a new object: no contradiction is ever stored in the state, and yet the stored blank presents, in the reality the state indexes, as the alternatives co-present. Contradiction and superposition come apart cleanly, and the entire measurement problem, on this diagnosis, lived in their conflation.

Postulate 1. Anything is real only relative to an Aethus. There is no view from nowhere against which your indexed picture is a mere approximation; the index is not a limitation on the description — it is the description's fundamental parameter. The site's own compression is worth adopting here: determinacy(q | A) — a two-place relation, never a property of q alone. And a rabbit fixes what this does not mean. Kevin sits in a field; a hawk circles. Kevin's chance of being eaten differs from the hawk's assessment of it — so there is no Platonic, camera-above-everything answer. But Kevin's chance is utterly indifferent to Kevin's opinions: a confident rabbit and a terrified rabbit are equally catchable, so this is no Protagorean relativism either. What remains is the framework's actual position — perspectival realism: indexed to an information state, and structurally constrained once indexed, the way a speed is always relative to something and yet never a matter of taste.

Postulate 2. Relative to a valid Aethus, an attribute is in superposition exactly when it is blank. This is the radical one, so state it at full strength: blankness is not ignorance of a fact. Blankness is the fact. Where your state carries no value, the world relative to you carries the open alternatives as constituents — real, co-present, weighted structure occupying this space. (The early papers phrased the condition as statistical independence; blankness is the mature, more primitive dress of the same content.)

The extrusion principle, and what happens when you learn

The extrusion principle is the move from Level 1 made ontological. Each resolution of a blank is a transition between complete, permanent block universes. The one you leave — in which the attribute was genuinely open — is not erased or falsified; it stands, walled off, no longer yours. The one you enter has the value written in tenselessly: in the heads-universe, the coin was always heads, all the way back. The framework calls your walk through this sequence centric unfolding, and it distinguishes the dark multiverse (the open alternatives co-present in your current structure — real, ahead of you, so to speak) from the lost multiverse (the structures your transitions have walled off — real, permanent, behind you). Nothing ever splits, and nothing ever travels between pockets: the only thing that moves is the index.

Notice what this deletes. The question "but which one was it really, before you looked?" presupposes exactly the unindexed stamp Postulate 1 removed. And notice what it keeps rigid: permanence. Once an attribute is stated in your Aethus, no later transition unsays it. History, relative to you, is immutable — and also, wherever it was never checkable, indeterminate. Immutable and open are compatible because settledness was never a property of pastness.

Two kinds of superposition — the framework's signature distinction

Not all open structure composes the same way, and the difference carries the physics.

In a disagreeing superposition, the alternatives carry their mutual exclusions internally: one pocket says "heads, and not tails," the other says "tails, and not heads." The pockets are co-present but non-interacting — nested, negation-separated block-universe candidates sharing one physical space. This is the generic case: your covered coin, your friend's location, Schrödinger's cat. In an agreeing superposition, the alternatives are composed without interior negations — a flat union, one pocket, the alternatives standing together with nothing stated against each other. This is the exotic case, and it is where quantum interference lives: the double slit's unwatched particle holds slit A and slit B in agreeing composition, and the interference pattern is that composition's empirical fingerprint. The algebra of the distinction fits in one line: (A and not-B) or (B and not-A) is not the same object as (A and B) — and the framework's claim is that classical thought only ever had the first, mistaking it for the nature of "or" itself.

The boundary, relocated

Why is your coin disagreeing while the particle is agreeing? Walk the framework's founding thought experiment: rebuild the double slit with a hundred people walking through two doors, their door assignments quantum-generated, the observer blindfolded. Will the wall show interference? No — and the reason is the whole theory. With people, clues are everywhere: you could ask them, check footprints, interview bystanders. Somewhere, some accessible chain of information distinguishes the alternatives — and that is enough to force the exclusions inside, collapsing the composition to the disagreeing kind. With the particle, nothing in the universe, even in principle, carries which-slit information — and only that total in-principle silence lets the agreeing composition stand. The quantum/classical boundary is informational, not physical: it moves when you add a detector, and it never moves because something got bigger, heavier, or more conscious.

Postulate 3 is what enforces this, and it is the framework's engine. In loose form: a valid Aethus must have at least one child state — one way of resolving into the future — all of whose own children remain consistent with what stands. Two generations deep, not one: an alternative isn't killed by being false now; it's killed when every way of continuing it runs into contradiction — checkmate, not capture. This pruning is what converts "the clues are out there" into "the superposition cannot be agreeing": if any accessible future could contradict a component, the composition holding that component uncontradicted is invalid, and the structure reduces. Collapse, in this framework, is nothing but pruning read through a transition. (Why two generations rather than one is a real derivation with a real history — the one-generation and ancestral versions each fail for stated reasons — and Level 5 walks it.)

What the rules buy immediately

Schrödinger's cat, without paradox: the cat is in disagreeing superposition relative to the outside experimenter — decoherence inside the box plays the detector's role, so the agreeing option is pruned — which means "alive and dead at once" was always the wrong reading; two negation-separated pockets, one of which the opening of the box indexes you into, retroactively-tenselessly. Wigner and his friend each hold correct, complete descriptions relative to their own Aethae, with a consistency coupling (Level 4) guaranteeing their reports can never be caught disagreeing. Your unread email, the far side of the Moon at this instant, everything unrecorded — all of it open structure, almost all of it disagreeing, none of it spooky. And a first taste of the framework's reach beyond measurement: flip a switch that time-reverses the outside universe, and the framework predicts events would not run backward — velocities kick, then entropy keeps increasing — because the second law rides your centric unfolding, not the coordinate clock. That is the entropic principle, and it's the sort of consequence you get once determinacy is indexed to information states rather than to time.

Three miniatures that make it concrete

Zoe's password makes the divergence quantitative in a toy. Zoe must crack a five-option password and owns a device that jumps her back five minutes. Classically she runs elimination — wrong guesses accumulate as knowledge, expected success in about three tries. Aethically, each jump returns her to a block universe in which the answer's attribute is blank again relative to her — the entry re-rolls — and she faces a fresh one-in-five each round: five expected tries, geometrically distributed, not three. Two ontologies, two different numbers, one toy.

Jenny's Japanese runs the same machinery at identity, and it is the framework's most unsettling licensed hypothetical. Your lifelong friend Jenny stuns her thirtieth birthday party by speaking fluent Japanese — a secret twenty-year project, she explains afterward. That night you accidentally trigger Zoe's device and land three hours back, before the reveal. The secret was never transferred to you — so relative to you it is blank again, and it re-rolls: at the candles, Jenny blinks at Japanese... and answers your brother-in-law's Italian fluently. Rewind further and progressively more of her unshared interior re-opens — identity interpolating, Ship-of-Theseus style, rather than flipping all-or-nothing. Read it honestly: this is an imagined-Aethus evaluation of a stipulated hypothetical, not a claim that such devices exist — its work is to dramatize that blanks lack tense: what was never checkable about the past was never settled, however long ago it "happened."

Monty Hall, finally, previews the probability story: lay out the alternatives, cross off what the host's reveal contradicts, redistribute the weight over what survives. On this framework that is not a trick for one puzzle — it is what probability is: weighted structure under pruning. The full algebra lives at Level 4.

What is not being claimed

No signaling: the consistency machinery forbids using any of this to transmit information. No consciousness-collapses-anything: a rock's isotope record indexes as well as a mind does. No retrocausation: what looks like backward influence is a tenseless consistency constraint misread through a timeline. No many-worlds splitting: pockets never multiply by dynamics; the count of alternatives only ever goes down, by pruning and by your transitions. And no free pass: the framework binds itself to falsifiable posture where it can — Level 4 shows where data already had a vote, and Level 5 lists what remains open on the framework's own books.

For the philosopher: modality brought indoors

The containment inversion

Every standing treatment of alternatives — Lewis's concrete worlds, Everett's branches, branching-time logics, the growing block, ordinary tense talk — shares one architectural commitment so old it is rarely stated: the completed world is the unit of alternative-hood. Alternatives are other ways the whole might be; the philosophical work then goes into the relation between the units (concreteness, branching, accessibility). Aethic reasoning inverts the containment: alternatives are entries inside the observer's information state, and the completed world — the finished history, Lewis's world, Everett's branch — is demoted from unit to ill-formed idealization. Nothing in the framework quantifies over completed histories; the papers decline them as objects rather than merely avoiding them. Indexicality is thereby promoted from a feature of reference to the architecture of modality itself. (From this vantage, Lewis and Everett read as one move in two vocabularies — worlds multiplied for modality, branches multiplied for unitarity, one inherited unit under both; the genealogy page's closing sections argue why that sameness is nearly invisible from inside either camp, and what stands checkable regardless.)

Two immediate consequences discipline everything downstream. Modal collapse of the middle category: the framework recognizes co-present alternatives (real, weighted, in the structure) and invalid ones (pruned, weight zero, retained only as labels) — and nothing else. "Mere possibility," the merely-conceivable-but-unreal, has no address; what survives pruning is in the room, and what doesn't is nowhere. Blanks lack tense: an attribute's openness is a property of the indexing state, not of any clock reading — the indiscriminate time principle — so the deep past can be exactly as open as next Tuesday, wherever it was never checkable, and "before" and "after" split into two non-redundant indices (coordinate time, ordering events within a block; Aethic time, ordering your resolutions across blocks) with only a loose, entropic rather than categorical monotonicity coupling them.

The counterfactual semantics: deletion, not refinement

The framework's first extraction paper does the modal work with no physics in the room, and its central act is a deletion. Lewis's similarity ordering — the graded ranking of worlds against actuality that his clauses quantify over — "appears nowhere: its work split across dependency structure, validity, and weight." In its place, the perturbation operator: to evaluate "had A been the case," you never leave the evaluating Aethus. Reopen the antecedent's entry in your own books at a declared scope; restate it to the counterfactual value; hold an explicit informational boundary fixed (the entries the evaluation keeps, stated as a parameter rather than smuggled as "similarity"); re-run the second- and third-postulate pruning over the result. What survives is the residue — a weighted, generally multi-pocket structure — and the clauses read directly off it: would is truth on every non-negligible pocket; might is truth on some non-null pocket; the graded counterfactual is the residue weight itself. One operator, three grains; chancy counterfactuals are first-class citizens rather than an afterthought.

Three features distinguish this from the neighbors. Against Lewis–Stalnaker: the ordering is deleted, and the framework stakes a standing formal challenge that no ordering semantics generates the indexed, weighted, multi-pocket verdict function — with the classification argument (a Cautious Monotony countermodel places the Aethic conditional outside the preferential family that similarity semantics lives in) as the down payment. Against structural-equations/interventionism (Pearl, Woodward, Halpern): the perturbation is intervention-shaped — clear, set, re-run — but the graph consulted is the evaluator's own stated content (deductive-and-causal-as-stated, provenance from the books rather than the modeler), the reopening is tense-free (any point in the block, no "before the miracle" rigidity), and the output is a weighted residue rather than a single surgically revised model. Against the epistemic readings: verdicts are evaluated at states, never stored in them as entries — conditionals are not bookable attributes — which is what keeps the Ramsey-test triviality results from getting started. Two structural theses ride along. Counterfactual substance without definiteness: the framework grants counterfactuals ontological standing (they are structure in the room) while denying them classical definiteness — the combination Fine's theorem punishes every classical-realist rival for lacking. And evaluation is synchronic in both temporal directions: the semantics never walks the unfolding backward to a branch point, and projection never walks it forward through a chain — the tree consulted as structure, never as itinerary.

Causation on two layers, and Reichenbach as a theorem

The causation extraction runs the same relocation at the classical triad — causes precede effects, causes screen off, no action at a distance — and its finding is that the triad was three stories about two layers. The information layer is tensed, light-cone-respecting, where signaling lives and relativity legislates. The validity layer is tenseless and absolute: reduced-form couplings — consistency constraints surviving the pruning — that bind attributes across any separation without carrying one bit. No causal arrow appears anywhere in the analysis; the two causal "horns" are defined by their behavior under conditioning, and with that definition Reichenbach's common-cause principle becomes a theorem — the screening-off certificate a one-line factorization — valid exactly up to the regime where alternatives co-obtain. The Bell correlations sit precisely at that boundary: not a violation of causal reasoning but the marker of where the single-layer assumption's writ runs out. This is the framework's direct answer to the Wood–Spekkens diagnosis — the fine-tuning no-go that afflicts causal accounts of Bell correlations assumes the one-layer picture the bilayer declines — and it is why apparent retrocausality (delayed choice included) dissolves: a tenseless validity constraint, gathered in whatever proper-time order, was being narrated as a signal.

The union principle: a sister taxon in logic

The logic extraction states the framework's deepest structural claim in a physics-quarantined form. Mutual exclusivity is not the nature of "or"; it is stated content — negations carried on the interior of each alternative — and therefore an achievement, and therefore removable. Decline to write the interior negations and a second composition regime falls out: alternatives standing together with nothing stated against each other, fixed against the classical regime by a one-statement test (writing in a single negation changes the composed object, which no mere gain of information about an antecedently exclusive situation could do). The claim at full taxonomic strength: this joint regime is a sister taxon to the entire classical catalogue of alternatives — "indecomposable without being simple," elemental yet internally structured — not a variant or a limiting case of anything catalogued. Interior silence is compositional determinacy, not indeterminacy: the absence of negations is part of the composition's content. The coordinates against the neighbors are stated, not gestured: quantum logic obtains its non-classical disjunction by revising the connectives (surrendering distributivity); here no connective is touched and classical logic holds throughout — what varies is noncontradiction's domain. The determinable-based indeterminacy literature is credited as the nearest family — it characterizes the single open state well — and charged with lacking exactly the missing chapter this paper supplies: the composition story, the algebra of open alternatives standing together, the boundary between the two forms. Physics enters only as an addendum: the agreeing superposition is this regime's instance in nature, and interference its fingerprint — the "or" of the unwatched double slit realizing, physically, as an "and."

For the logician, the cumulative effect indexes as eight load-bearing departures from sentential logic, each enabling the next: (1) the unit of fact — stated-attributes with dependency structure, not propositions; (2) the layered container — states within Aethae, not sentences in a valuation; (3) the formal/empirical separation — the apparatus defined prior to, and only then confronted with, observation; (4) blankness as a constructive primitive rather than any negation; (5) disjunction split into the two composition regimes; (6) conjunction made directional by the assertion structure; (7) contradiction transposed from a syntactic clash to a structural, propagating invalidity; (8) static truth-tables replaced by the third postulate's dynamic validity check. The difference from Boolean practice is operational, not stylistic — and the classical behavior is recovered exactly where the classical assumptions hold.

Truth, logic's two faces, and identity

Three shorter theses complete the philosophical frame. Truth as an intersection-test outcome: a claim's truth, relative to an Aethus, is read off the validity of intersecting its content with the state — degrees of abstraction handled by what the intersection is taken against, which is how the framework keeps realism about logic while indexing everything else (the worked showpiece: "Batman exists" receives a graded, population-indexed verdict rather than a shrug). The dual logic principle: abstract logic (unindexed implication among contents) and causal logic (what pruning enforces in the world's structure) are distinguished and then systematically related — the framework's answer to why logic seems both about thought and about the world. And identity without rigid designation: the possible-worlds-to-Aethae switch denies the cross-world "categorical substance" rigid designators would point to; Kripke's linguistics survives untouched while his metaphysical presupposition is declined, and task-objects — objects individuated by a contribution relative to a designated root Aethus — become fundamental, with rigid-designator talk licensed as shorthand. Perturb Aristotle and his identity measurably shifts; whether he is "still Aristotle" is a question about which task-Aristotle your discourse designated.

Decision theory: the fifty-year deadlock's shared premise

The Newcomb extraction is the cleanest self-contained payoff. Causal and evidential decision theory have contested the predictor problem for half a century while jointly granting one premise: the prediction, being past, is determinate at the moment you choose. The framework prices the premise instead of presupposing it: an unread outcome is a blank entry, and blanks lack tense — so the prediction's content, relative to the chooser, is open, coupled to the choice by the validity layer rather than by any backward signal. One-boxing follows without news value and without retrocausation, in the zero-error limit outright and above a stated accuracy threshold in general. The same instrument — a binding criterion sorting which act-outcome couplings are act-robust — then runs the standard battery: one-box on Newcomb, smoke on the smoking lesion (the lesion coupling is not act-robust), two-box on the transparent variant, which is exactly where this framework and functional decision theory part company, with the divergence stated rather than smoothed. A second, independent route reaches the same verdict through the anthropic wing (the predictor's track record as reference-class information under the Accordance Principle), and the convergence of a modal route and a typicality route on one verdict is offered as evidence the move is structural. The diagnosis generalizes: the standard apparatus is the Aethic one with the past artificially completed, "and its familiar paradoxes are manufactured at exactly the point of completion."

Where this sits, and what a decliner keeps

Comparisons, stated with their deltas: RQM shares the relational indexing but keeps event-relative facts without the permanence, the pruning engine, or the modal semantics; QBism shares the agent-centering but reads states doxastically where Aethic states are ontic and non-doxastic (the framework's 2023 QBism dress was an on-ramp, retired); Everett shares the realism about superposition but branches at spacetime where this framework "branches" — misleadingly said — at the observer's Aethus, with nothing ever splitting; Tappenden's Unitary Mind and the Carroll–Sebens separability principle are credited as the literature's closest approach, blocked from the full inversion by many-worlds' objective branch points; Müller's algorithmic idealism is engaged with a two-way trade (the weighted layer answers the branch-weight problem; the attribute-grain tracking is offered back). And the honesty clause the corpus itself insists on: every collapse-of-a-debate claimed above is conditional on the inversion. A reader who declines observer-indexed determinacy keeps Lewis, keeps the CDT/EDT standoff, keeps the triad — and keeps, unconditionally, only the structural observations (shared quantification over completed histories; shared premises never contested) that are checkable against the texts with no Aethic commitment in hand.

LEVEL 4Assumes quantum-mechanics literacy. Leaves you with the measurement analysis, the Nexus, the boundary theorem, and the falsifiable joints.

For the physicist: measurement without a transition

The classification, before any dynamics

Start where the third extraction paper starts: with no Hilbert space, no dynamics, no scale parameter — only the three consistency postulates on observer-indexed information states — and derive what "quantum versus classical" even sorts. The result is a classification theorem: every candidate superposition falls into one of three bins. Valid agreeing compositions (the alternatives jointly uncontradictable by any in-principle-accessible information — the quantum case). Valid disagreeing compositions (exclusions forced interior by accessible distinguishing chains — the generic macroscopic case). Invalid compositions (pruned outright). The boundary between the first two is a discrete informational fact that moves with the detectors — never with mass, size, temperature, or consciousness — and the paper inventories on its face what is posited versus derived, with exactly one flagged axiom purchasing the standard interference statistics (the Feynman weighting; more below). Everything in this level is that classification cashed against the standard puzzles. And the classification generalizes past physics on its face: wherever information is hidden in principle, the framework asserts agreeing structure — alternatives interacting — in any domain whatsoever; one contrary observation anywhere kills it.

The community's goalposts

In 2022 Hance and Hossenfelder published five requirements any genuine solution to the measurement problem must meet — the physics community's goalposts, not this framework's. Where each is addressed here, with grading carried from the papers: R1 (agree with all existing data) — the deflation argument and the status verdict below: equivalent where tested, with the divergences named rather than hidden. R2 (reproduce quantum mechanics, collapse postulate and Born rule included, in a well-defined limit) — the Quantum Nexus supplies the interference statistics with its one axiom flagged; the Born rule arrives through the weight–probability homomorphism; collapse is reproduced as pruning read through a transition, exactly and only where the standard theory deploys it. R3 (say unambiguously what a measurement device is) — the classification theorem's cleanest dividend: a measurement device is any in-principle-accessible record-former — detector, environment, colleague — with no further properties required; unambiguous precisely because it is informational and detector-relative rather than dynamical. R4 (reproduce classical physics in a well-defined limit) — the disagreeing regime is that limit's structure, the fundamental theorem gives its mechanism (macroscopic attributes stated by excess of deductive avenues), and the remaining macroscopic-regime work is booked on the open ledger rather than claimed. R5 (reconcile nonlocal collapse with local conservation) — dissolved rather than balanced: no dynamical collapse process exists to conflict with anything; observation is re-indexing, the nonlocal correlations ride the tenseless validity layer, and the information layer — where conservation and signaling live — is never touched.

Measurement as two steps; determinate nodes as boundary conditions

The Aethic Sifting Principle replaces the measurement event with two separated operations: measurement is the agreeing-to-disagreeing structural transition (forced wherever accessibility appears — a detector, decoherence into an accessible environment, a friend inside a box), and observation is the subsequent pocket selection (your transition into one block universe of the disagreeing set). Nothing dynamical happens at observation; the continuity desideratum a century of interpretation co-signed — a persisting medium carrying the state through collapse — is demoted as a category error rather than satisfied. The structural residue of measurements is the framework's key physical object: a determinate node — a locus whose attribute the pruning has fixed (or mutually-exclusively partitioned) relative to any observer whose Aethus constrains the corresponding measurement. Determinate nodes function as Aethic boundary conditions: the intermediate quantum structure is not independently posited but is whatever survives the reduced integral threading between the nodes — which displaces the background time parameter as the fundamental sequencing layer, and does so foliation-independently, since nodes are defined against spacetime proper.

The Quantum Nexus

The framework's path-integral face renders the one flagged axiom explicit. Probability having been identified with Aethic weight, the weight on the Aethus in which a given subset of paths is taken in agreeing superposition is written as an Aethus-valued integral over the powerset of all paths:

\[ N \;\propto\; \int_{\mathcal{P}(X)} \iint A(T)\, \chi_T(q_1)\, \chi_T(q_2)\, \cos\!\Big( \tfrac{1}{\hbar}\big( S[q_1(t)] - S[q_2(t)] \big) \Big)\, \mathcal{D}[q_2(t)]\, \mathcal{D}[q_1(t)]\, d\mu(T) \]

Read it as the framework does. \(X\) is the exhaustive set of continuous, simply connected paths — feasibility deliberately not presupposed, since "discounting impossibilities is the work of the subsequent reduced form." \(T\) ranges over the powerset; \(A(T)\) is the Aethus asserting that exactly the paths in \(T\) are taken in agreeing superposition — the term that makes the whole integral an Aethus-valued object, so that Aethic pruning can act directly on the summed terms. The cosine of the action difference is the Feynman weighting, and its status is stated with unusual bluntness: it is axiomatic at this stage — the framework's one purchased empirical input, booked as an Open Construction (the "kernel") rather than smuggled as derived. An arbitrary quantum system's Aethus is then the reduced form of the intersection of this Quantum Nexus with an Aethus holding the initial conditions and whatever else is stated. Observables fall out as conventions — selections of determinately valued attributes relative to an Aethus — rather than derivational primitives.

The powerset invalidation, worked

The classification theorem's engine is a counting argument any reader can re-run. Take four slits with detectors that cannot distinguish slits within class \(E_1 = \{1,2\}\) or within \(E_2 = \{3,4\}\), but can distinguish across the classes. Then of the \(2^4 - 1 = 15\) non-empty path-subsets: exactly \(k = 2\) are valid (the full classes — agreeing superposition within a class); \( \sum_j (2^{|E_j|} - 2) = 4 \) are pruned by the second postulate (proper subsets of a class assert a specificity no Aethus could ever support — the which-slit attribute within a class being permanently blank); and the remaining \(9\) are pruned by the third postulate (subsets spanning classes admit a child Aethus — a detector firing — that contradicts them: "Aethic checkmate"). The preferred basis thereby arrives informationally: the detector configuration, not a dynamical superselection, fixes which decomposition survives. Slide the detectors and the boundary slides with them — which is the theorem's standing falsifiable edge: exhibit interference across in-principle-distinguishable alternatives and the framework is dead.
