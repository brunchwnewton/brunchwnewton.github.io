How Aethic
reasoning works.

The framework built up one layer at a time — from the single move to the Quantum Nexus — with the central theorem rendered as a demo you can operate yourself. Read it in plain language, or switch on the formalism.

Every layer below is the same one sentence, read at a new site: determinacy is a two-place relation between an attribute and an information state, never a property a fact carries on its own. Nothing is added as the layers stack; the consequences are just followed further.

L0

The move

Physics pins facts to spacetime: one complete list of what is the case, the same for everyone, attached to points. The move is to stop doing that — to index reality to an observer’s complete information state, the Aethus, and follow the consequence. The block universe survives untouched; what varies is which of its co-present decompositions is yours.

In one line

Whether something is definite is not a fact about the thing — it is a fact about the thing together with who is asking.

the relationdeterminacy( q ∣ A )  — a two-place relation over an attribute q and an Aethus A. There is no A-free ledger; a fact with no Aethus to carry it is a category error.
L1

The Aethus and its attributes

An Aethus is the complete set of facts-for-an-observer, closed under deduction. Its units are attributes — question-shaped pieces of description (where is the car parked; which slit did the electron take). An attribute is stated when the Aethus carries its answer and blank when it does not.

objectsAn attribute q is a question with an answer space. An Aethus A is a deductively closed assignment: A(q) = v when q is stated with value v, A(q) = ∗ when blank. Closure: if the stated entries entail q = v, then A(q) = v.

Four terms of art, and no more: Aethus, attribute, superposition (what a blank amounts to), and reduced form (the residue that survives the consistency constraints). Everything else is plain English.

L2

Blanks are tense-free and constructive

Two features do the early work. First, blankness has nothing to do with time: your unlearned past is exactly as open as your unformed future. A road eroding unobserved for decades is blank — and so genuinely open — relative to everyone who hasn’t learned of it, resolving when someone finds out, not when it collapses.

Second, and load-bearing: a blank is not a hidden answer. It is a complete entry in its own right, with no determinate value waiting underneath. The habit of hearing “unknown” as “determinate but unrevealed” is exactly the habit the framework breaks — and the next layer shows the break is forced, not stylistic.

L3

Counterfactuals as an operation, not a journey

To evaluate “if X had happened, Y would have,” you don’t travel to another world. You operate on your own books: reopen the antecedent (return it to blank, with what rests on it), restate it to the supposed value, and re-prune — let the consistency constraints eliminate what can no longer stand — then read the residue.

Read at three grains, the residue gives three everyday notions: would (every survivor settles Y), might (some survivor does), and probability (the weighted fraction that does). One operation, three resolutions — and the fifty-year-old “similarity ordering” of the standard theory appears nowhere. It is deleted, not refined; its work split among the books’ own dependency structure, validity, and weight.

the perturbation operatorπX(A) = R( (A ⊖ qX) + {qX = x} )

A ⊨ X □→ Y  iff every pocket of πX(A) settles Y  (would)
A ⊨ X ◇→ Y  iff some pocket does  (might)
PA(Y ∥ X) = w(πX(A) ↾ Y) / w(πX(A))  (probability)
L4

The union move: the secret life of “or”

If the alternatives to a blank are genuinely co-present, why does anyone see just one? The answer reforms “or.” Listing a blank’s alternatives is one job; whether they interact is a separate job, handled by what’s written inside each alternative, not by a wall between them.

Where each alternative states, internally, that it doesn’t interact with the others, they are co-present but inert — a disagreeing superposition, the ordinary case. Where that interior entry is left blank, they genuinely interact — an agreeing superposition. One attribute’s worth of difference, carrying the entire classical–quantum divide.

where the negations sit is the whole contentagreeing:  ⋃i Ai     disagreeing:  ⋃i ( Ai ⋂ ⋃j≠i ¬Aj )

So there is no physical “or” holding things apart — only co-presence, with or without interior negations. Interaction is the default; exclusivity is manufactured. Quantum interference is what the world looks like when the interior negations are absent, and the classical either/or is the logic of the reduced form — the shadow, not the object.

L5

The three postulates, as codification

Nothing here is a new request — the postulates codify rules already in use. P1 (Indexing): every reality-attribution is of the form “q takes value v relative to A.” P2 (Blanks): an attribute is in superposition relative to A exactly when it is blank in A. P3 (Pruning): an alternative survives only if it can be consistently refined; those that can’t are not improbable but invalid.

P3, stated with care (two-generational)V[A] ⇒ ∃B ⊑ A, ∀C ⊑ B, V[C]
a valid Aethus must have a refinement all of whose refinements are valid
L6

Checkmate: collapse as a theorem

Why does the pruning take the two-generational shape? Run the double slit in a macroscopic costume — blindfolded organizer, participants secretly assigned doors. The organizer’s which-door attributes are blank, so P2 puts them in superposition; yet the organizer is guaranteed to see two clusters, never interference. The rule that delivers this is found by elimination: a one-generation existential rule condemns innocent siblings (invalidity would flow child-to-parent); a one-generation universal rule never fires. Folding in the fact that these states are self-similar yields the two-generational alternation.

The chess reading, literally

Check is an escapable possibility of contradiction; checkmate is the impossibility of escaping it; and checkmate is invalidity. The postulate says, in four words: it must not be impossible to make contradiction impossible.

fixed-point formD = μX. ( Base ∪ { A : ∀B ⊑ A, ∃C ⊑ B, C ∈ X } )
the doomed set as a least fixed point — a safety property in the modal μ-calculus; checkmate is membership in D. Knaster–Tarski guarantees it exists for arbitrary branching.
L7

The climax: the boundary is informational

Take a particle with several available paths, and instead of considering them one at a time, consider the powerset — every combination held together as a candidate for co-present content. Give each slit its detector signature (which detectors fire if it alone is taken); identical signatures are permanently indistinguishable, sorting the slits into classes. The theorem sorts every combination into exactly three bins.

The consequence is the framework’s central physical claim: move a detector and you redraw the signatures, which re-sorts the powerset. The quantum–classical boundary is literal re-classification — drawn by what the apparatus renders distinguishable, never by how big anything is. Operate it yourself:

With no detectors there is one class and the only valid combination is the full set — pure interference. With a detector on every slit, each singleton is its own class and every multi-slit combination is checkmated — the pure particle pattern. Every arrangement in between slides the boundary continuously. The preferred basis you were never told how to pick falls out for free: it is the detector-induced partition itself.

L8

One machine: from game shows to entanglement

When the pruning eliminates joint configurations of several attributes, the survivors show a correlation nobody put in by hand — reduced-form coupling. Run it on Monty Hall: the host’s rule bakes in two contradictions (never your door, never the car’s), so those joint configurations never enter the structure; when he opens a door, you cross out the inconsistent survivors and the crossed-out weight is simply gone, the rest rescaling. Switching wins two-thirds — and the general fact is that Bayesian conditioning is pruning-plus-renormalization, a theorem rather than an added rule.

Now change only the weights — swap in the quantum amplitude kernel — and the identical operation becomes entanglement (Bell correlations are the surviving joint outcomes no future Aethus can catch in a contradiction) and, run across observers, Wigner’s-friend consistency (the observers who can meet are exactly the ones who agree). One operation; four literatures — and the reduced form was one deductive step from the postulates, the unification falling out afterward.

L9

The Quantum Nexus: the one axiom that buys the numbers

Everything so far ran on the three postulates and enumeration alone. The numbers enter through exactly one further axiom, flagged in bright ink: the Quantum Nexus, the Feynman kernel installed as the weight structure over the powerset of paths. It is separable — remove it and the whole ontology still stands; install it and standard quantum statistics arrive (Malus’s law becomes a theorem, polarizer chains fall out as sequential pruning). Its own derivation is assigned, explicitly, to a successor program — the position Newton’s law of gravitation held beside his three laws.

the Nexus (factored form)W(T) = | Σp∈T K(p) |²,   K(p) = eiS[p]/ℏ    N ∝ ∫P(X) W(T) A(T) dν(T)
A(T) is the Aethus in which the paths of T run in agreeing superposition — the kill-switch: invalid clusters zero out. Interference is carried by the kernel’s cross-terms, never by any fraction of validity, so no negative weight ever hides.

Installing it settles one more thing: the wavefunction is demoted to derived bookkeeping over an agreeing superposition. Once nothing fundamental persists through measurement, there is no transition to trigger. Agreeing superpositions never collapse; a record just invalidates them whole, and measurement’s randomness lives one level up, in the disagreeing superposition over outcomes. Predetermination per information-state, randomness across them.

L10

The block universe, a new axis

The block universe survives — one objective four-dimensional reality, exactly as relativity delivers it — and becomes categorically stranger: within the one block, multiple determinate realities are simultaneously legitimate, each indexed to an information state, none outranking the others.

Not classical realism — there is no single catalogue of facts. Not idealism — no mind creates anything. A third setting of the dial: reality independent, determinacy indexed.

The traditional debates in the metaphysics of spacetime — substance versus relation, present versus eternal — are old axes. This proposes a new one rather than taking a side on an old one. And the base-rate skepticism any new interpretation deserves inverts into something sharper: the central problem of every leading interpretation falls to the same two moves — the informational boundary, and the observer indexing. Evidence, at least, about where the difficulty always lived.
