Glossary

Every framework-specific term used in the Aethic and Nexic reasoning guides, defined in one or two sentences with cross-references to the layer where each appears.

A
Accordance Principle Nexic
For a given attribute A of Earth’s history, the ratio P(H|A′)/P(H|A) is bounded by P(A)/(1−P(A)). The deductive engine of Nexic reasoning: it constrains how much better or worse an alternative history could be for producing observers, without requiring direct knowledge of that alternative.
Nexic Layer 2
Accordance Theorem Nexic
Self-mediocrity, cosmic-mediocrity, and the Copernican principle cannot all be true simultaneously. Since the first two are well-supported, the Copernican principle breaks, yielding the Rare Earth hypothesis as a derivation.
Nexic Layer 8
Accordance Timescale (ATS) Nexic
An alternative to the Geologic Timescale tailored to the direct experiences of our ancestral species and lineage, made possible by the Accordance Principle.
Nexic Layer 6
Aetherian / Aetherian Nexus Aethic Nexic
An Aetherian is an observer whose Aethus qualifies as mediocre under the Aethic mediocrity principle. The Aetherian Nexus is the weighted superposition of all Aethae weighted by observer-existence probability.
Aethic Layer 5 · Nexic Layer 5
Aetherian Independence Principle Nexic
The Cosmic Nexus is statistically independent of the Aetherian Nexus. The universe’s volumetric frequency of configurations has no built-in correlation with observer-existence probability. This is the bridge principle that allows the Fundamental Theorem of Nexic Reasoning to produce the Optimal-Earth Conjecture.
Nexic Layer 3
Aethic Alternate History Theorem Aethic Nexic
Any two agent-associated Aethae share a common parent Aethus somewhere in their Aethic past, so the world of either can be represented as an "alternate history" of the other. This dissolves the dividing line between "real Earth variants" and "other planets" (including fictional ones): because an Aethic path via parent and child Aethae always exists between any two Aetherian worlds, the distinction is structural rather than categorical. Provides the scaffolding for the Cosmic Accordance Principle and the Open Cosmos.
Nexic Layer 1
Aethic Base Principle of Superpositions Aethic
An agent’s consciousness occupies whichever block universe maximizes surrounding superposition content without alerting them to a discernible difference between any two states in superposition. This is the conceptual predecessor to the Second Postulate; the Second Postulate provides the mathematical grounding.
Aethic Layer 4
Aethic Coherence Principle Aethic
An empirical principle stating that a certain class of Aethic attributes follows a predictable agreeing superposition under the relevant context. Every Aethic uncertainty principle has a corresponding coherence principle: wherever information is permanently hidden, the possible states interact. This is one half of the Fundamental Theorem.
Aethic Layer 9
Aethic Contradiction Aethic
An Aethic intersection of disjoint Aethae — conflicting information asserted simultaneously — which produces an invalid Aethus. Structurally distinct from superposition: a contradiction is an intersection of conflicting content, while a superposition is a union of withheld information. The Union Principle's decoupling of logical from physical makes this distinction possible, and the Third Postulate operates on the first without disturbing the second.
Aethic Layer 6
Aethic Dimension Aethic
An additional degree of freedom orthogonal to all four spacetime dimensions, along which the Markov chain of block universes extends. Not a spatial dimension but a qualitative one — the axis along which informational transitions occur.
Aethic Layer 1
Aethic Equivalence Aethic
Two static Aethae are Aethically equivalent if they are fully derivable from one another — two different ways of writing the same information content. The Aethus itself is the equivalence class of static Aethae under Aethic equivalence. This is what gives Aethae their coordinate-independence, in the same way that a tensor is independent of the coordinate system used to express it.
Aethic Layer 3
Aethic Intersection Aethic
The multiplication operation in the Aethic ring. Adds information, moving toward child Aethae. Taking the intersection of two Aethae asserts that both sets of information hold simultaneously; if they conflict, the result is an invalid (contradictory) Aethus.
Aethic Layer 5
Aethic Markov Chain Aethic
The structural object the Extrusion Principle replaces single-timeline ontology with: a chain of complete, static, relativistic block universes, with observer-experienced time being movement along the chain rather than within any one of them. Each transition between blocks is driven by the acquisition of new information; each block is itself permanent and holds its Aethic superposition content indefinitely. The structural facet of the Extrusion Principle.
Aethic Layer 1
Aethic Mediocrity Principle Aethic Nexic
An Aethus B is mediocre in parent A if, under some probability distribution over a partition of A, B lies in the central quantile interval. The engine behind both self-mediocrity and cosmic mediocrity. Applied to oneself, it yields the postulate of self-mediocrity; applied to the cosmos, it yields the postulate of cosmic mediocrity.
Aethic Layer 5 · Nexic Layer 5
Aethic Multiverse Aethic
The multiverse that emerges from the qualitative facet of the Extrusion Principle: all alternate block universes occupy the same physical spacetime, distinguished only by their informational indexing to a given Aethus. Not branching, not spatially separated, not elsewhere — the alternate realities are here, layered into ladder-ontology pockets that share the same spatial substrate but cannot causally interact. The blur radius is right at the edge of your senses.
Aethic Layer 1
Aethic Partition Theorem Aethic
The union of all valid Aethae in a partition equals the union of all elements, since invalid Aethae contribute zero weight. This grounds the connection between the Aethic ring and classical probability: the total probability over a complete partition is always one.
Aethic Layer 5
Aethic Stratification Aethic
The structural arrangement under which observers with different information genuinely occupy different Aethae and therefore different block universes, with each correct about their situation relative to their own Aethus. The shape the Wigner’s Friend and Frauchiger–Renner setups leave behind once the First Postulate is in place: no single shared description forces conflict, because the observers’ predictions are about distinct Aethic block universes rather than a single shared one.
Aethic Layer 2
Aethic Superposition Aethic
The general term for states coexisting within a single block universe. Comes in two categorically distinct types: agreeing superposition (states can interact, interference preserved) and disagreeing superposition (states are copresent but cannot interact, yielding classical mutual exclusivity). Standard quantum superposition is a special case of agreeing superposition.
Aethic Layer 6
Aethic Sweeps Aethic
Environmental interactions that test whether an agreeing superposition can survive. Each sweep is an independent Bernoulli threat: if any information distinguishing the superposed states leaks into the environment, the Third Postulate fires and the superposition becomes disagreeing. The exponential decay of quantum coherence is the cumulative survival probability over vast numbers of sweeps.
Aethic Layer 10
Aethic Template Aethic
The analogue to an Aethus of what an attribute is to a stated-attribute. Where an Aethus can be stored in another Aethus as a stated-attribute, the Aethic Template is the corresponding attribute. This enables the hierarchical nesting of Aethae within Aethae.
Aethic Layer 3
Aethic Tree Aethic
The directed acyclic graph of parent-child relations between Aethae. Child Aethae inherit their parent's information and add more; Aethic time is traversal along this tree from less to more specified Aethae. The Null Aethus sits at the root as the superposition of all possibilities; maximally specified Aethae sit at the leaves. The postulates operate on this structure, and Aethic weighting (probability) is defined over it.
Aethic Layer 3
Aethic Truth Aethic
Observer-indexed but structurally constrained, independent of opinions or will. Not subjective (beliefs don’t determine reality) and not objective (there is no view from nowhere). A third option: reality is relational but not arbitrary. The Kevin the Rabbit thought experiment makes this precise.
Aethic Layer 2
Aethic Uncertainty Principle Aethic
An empirical principle corresponding to a class of Aethic attributes being permanently blank due to a related permanencible attribute. Heisenberg’s uncertainty principle is one specific instance. Each Aethic uncertainty principle generates a corresponding Aethic coherence principle via the Fundamental Theorem.
Aethic Layer 9
Aethic Union Aethic
The addition operation in the Aethic ring. Removes information, moving toward parent Aethae and producing superposition. Taking the union of two Aethae creates a new Aethus that knows only what both had in common.
Aethic Layer 5
Aethic Weight Aethic
A real-valued coefficient attached to an element of an Aethus, representing its degree of presence. Aethic weights interpolate between full presence (weight one) and full absence (weight zero), replacing the binary in-or-out membership of set theory with a continuous spectrum. These weights are the probability algebra.
Aethic Layer 5
Aethus (pl. Aethae) Aethic Nexic
The central mathematical object: an equivalence class of static Aethae encoding the totality of information accessible to a given observer. Each Aethus maps bijectively to a block universe (a reality). The Aethus is to Aethic reasoning what the reference frame is to special relativity.
Aethic Layer 3
Aethus-Reality Dualism Aethic
Principle 36: for any valid Aethus there is automatically a corresponding reality, and vice versa. This makes consciousness a matter of degree rather than kind. Any information-gathering system—from a flatworm to a human—has the grounds for a rudimentary Aethus.
Aethic Layer 2
Agreeing Superposition Aethic
States that are physically copresent and can interact (interfere). Arises when information distinguishing the states is permanently hidden. The elementary Aethic union of Aethae: ∪ᵢ Aᵢ. This is the quantum interference case in the double-slit experiment.
Aethic Layer 6
Anthropic Accordance Principle Nexic
The specific single-event derivation of the Accordance Principle: for a low-probability event A that we observe, the probability that A is an anthropic parameter is bounded below at approximately 1 − P(A). Distinct from the general Accordance Principle (the underlying mediocrity commitment) in being an operational rule for deriving anthropic bounds from specific observed flukes. Load-bearing for the Hațeg Island analysis and similar single-event inferences.
Nexic Layer 3
Anthropic Facet Nexic
The second facet of the Exotic Accordance Principle. Takes the regime classification (established by the Category Facet) as input and outputs the anthropic-necessity claim: established exotic structure in one’s block-universe past is almost surely a Prerequisite to observer-existence, because its presence would otherwise be a cosmic fluke. Conjoined with the Category Facet, produces the Miracle classification via the two-step inference.
Nexic Layer 2
Anthropic Parameter Nexic
Any feature of reality that served as a requisite for human existence. Physical anthropic parameters are numerical constants (gravitational constant, fine-structure constant). Circumstantial anthropic parameters are historical events (K-Pg extinction, endosymbiosis). Nexic reasoning’s founding insight is that no principled distinction separates the two.
Nexic Layer 1
Assembly-Index Bimodal Signature Nexic
The empirical signature the Exotic Principle predicts at the molecular scale: biological molecules and non-biological molecules cluster categorically separately on the assembly-index distribution (Sharma et al. 2023), with no smooth gradient between them. The molecular-scale version of the bimodal hazard-rate regime, treated by the framework as a scale-model projection of the same exotic-versus-common structure dichotomy that shows up at the natural-historical scale (K-Pg, eukaryotic merger, α-gal, Hațeg).
Nexic Layer 2
Attentor / Contentor Nexic
Two classes of selection pressure in Nexic reasoning. An attentor is a pressure that sharpens cognitive or sensory traits (e.g., theropod predation forcing vigilance). A contentor is a pressure that drives physical or metabolic competition. The distinction matters because only attentors drive the specific cognitive architecture required for conscious observers.
Nexic Layer 6
Attribute Cascade Aethic
The closure of a static Aethus under all logically derivable attributes. If Tim has a Dog and it’s a Golden Retriever, then “Tim’s pet’s breed” must be present. This converts a 2-Aethus into a 3-Aethus.
Aethic Layer 3
B
Bimodal Hazard-Rate Regime Nexic
The structural feature of Aethic hazard-rate distributions the Exotic Principle names: natural history under Optimation clusters into two categorically separated regimes — common structure (where most cosmic processes live) and exotic structure (where Miracle-class events live) — with the intermediate region almost empty. The underlying probabilistic substrate is continuous; the categorical separation is the empirical signature of how Optimation populates that substrate, not a discontinuity in the substrate itself.
Nexic Layer 2
Biological Hyperblock Nexic
The Cartesian product of spacetime and an Aethic dimension, giving Nexic reasoning a specific geometric construction on which to run spatial-mediocrity arguments. Under the Biological Hyperblock, "the special places" in anthropic reasoning becomes a well-defined geometric region (high-Aetherian-density subspace) rather than a vague instinct, and the Cosmic Accordance Principle becomes a precise mathematical statement of mediocrity over that region.
Nexic Layer 2
Biosphere Doomsday Pacing Nexic
The application of Doomsday-argument reasoning to entire biospheres rather than individual species. Under the Cosmic Nexus, most biospheres should be sterilized relatively early in their history. Earth’s 4-billion-year biosphere is therefore highly atypical—which is exactly what the Accordance Principle predicts for an observer-containing world.
Nexic Layer 7
Blank Attribute Aethic
An attribute not present in a static Aethus, representing unknowing. A directly blank attribute is physically absent. A conceptually blank attribute returns all possible states upon retrieval. The Second Postulate generates superposition from conceptually blank attributes.
Aethic Layer 3 · Layer 4
Block Universe Aethic
A complete, static, four-dimensional spacetime consistent with general relativity. In Aethic reasoning, reality is a Markov chain of block universes rather than a single one. Each node in the chain is a complete spacetime; the observer’s felt passage of time is movement along the chain.
Aethic Layer 1
Blur Radius Aethic
The pedagogical device for how close the Aethic Multiverse actually is. Conventional multiverse pictures place alternate realities far away — in parallel dimensions, at branching points, in distant corners of the cosmos. Aethic reasoning places them just past your line of sight: the people upstairs from you are already in disagreeing superposition, and the multiverse is fully present once you turn away from anything you aren't directly observing.
Aethic Layer 6
Born Rule Onset Principle Aethic
The Born rule (probability = |ψ|²) is applied after summing agreeing superpositions but before summing disagreeing superpositions. Agreeing states sum probability amplitudes (interference preserved); disagreeing states sum probability masses (classical addition).
Aethic Layer 10
C
Cancelled Flight Illustration Nexic
The framework’s canonical analogy for wretched evolution: a catastrophe that turns out to have opened the only door one could have walked through, the way a cancelled flight occasionally turns out to have saved the would-be passenger’s life. The K-Pg extinction is the framework’s flagship cancelled-flight case — an asteroid impact that, on its own merits, would read as a tragedy, but which retrospectively appears as the cancelled flight that routed our lineage through to the present.
Nexic Layer 6
Case C Aethic
The new prediction: macroscopic scenario interaction without wave behavior. Standard physics has one knob for the quantum-to-classical transition (scale). Aethic reasoning has two independent knobs (scale and information structure). Case C is what happens when scale is classical but information is permanently hidden. The billiard ball experiment is a Case C system.
Aethic Layer 9
Category Facet Nexic
The first facet of the Exotic Accordance Principle. Takes empirical improbability as input and outputs the regime classification: any extraordinarily improbable event occurring in the observer’s block-universe past must be exotic structure rather than ordinary common-structure variation, since common structure is defined by intrinsic-rate-matches-realized-rate and the established improbability is exactly the case where these come apart. Conjoined with the Anthropic Facet, produces the Miracle classification via the two-step inference.
Nexic Layer 2
Centric Unfolding Aethic
The process by which an observer moves along the Aethic dimension: gaining new information causes the Aethus to intersect with the new stated-attribute, extruding to a child Aethus. Every act of looking, learning, or measuring is centric unfolding.
Aethic Layer 5
Checkmate (vs. Check) Aethic
Check: a single child Aethus is invalid (one future leads to contradiction, but escape routes exist). Checkmate: every child Aethus has at least one invalid grandchild — no escape route exists. The Third Postulate invalidates an Aethus only on checkmate, not mere check.
Aethic Layer 7
Common Structure Nexic
Events whose intrinsic Cosmic-Nexus hazard rate approximately matches their realized frequency in the observer’s biosphere — the kind of structure standard physics, chemistry, and stellar dynamics produce by ordinary mechanisms. Paired with exotic structure as the two regimes the Exotic Principle names. Most of the universe consists of common structure; the empirical signature of exotic structure is precisely the gap between its intrinsic and realized frequencies.
Nexic Layer 2
Copernican Principle Nexic
The claim that Earth’s position and conditions are typical of the cosmos—that life should be common. The Accordance Theorem proves this must be false: self-mediocrity, cosmic mediocrity, and the Copernican principle cannot all hold simultaneously. Since the first two are well-supported, the Copernican principle is the one that breaks.
Nexic Layer 8
Cosmic Accordance Principle Nexic
The spatial-mediocrity form of the Accordance Principle: an Aetherian can see themselves as a uniformly random Aetherian within whatever subspace of the Biological Hyperblock they are given to occupy. Mathematically equivalent to the Anthropic Accordance Principle but derived through spatial mediocrity over the hyperblock rather than through the fluke-skepticism argument. Two independent derivations converging on the same inequality makes the underlying Accordance commitment more robust.
Nexic Layer 2
Cosmic Mediocrity Nexic
The postulate that the universe does not present a mediocre observer with events disproportionately frequently relative to their likelihoods. Combined with self-mediocrity, it generates the Accordance Theorem and thereby the Rare Earth derivation.
Nexic Layer 5
Cosmic Nexus Nexic
A Nexus where weights represent cosmic volumetric frequency — how common a given Aethus-configuration is across the universe. This drives the Accordance Principle.
Aethic Layer 5 · Nexic Layer 3
D
Dark Era Nexic
The Mesozoic period reinterpreted as an evolutionary crucible in which theropod predation pressure was causally necessary for mammalian cognitive architecture. The existence and subsequent extinction of dinosaurs are treated not as incidental but as prerequisite anthropic parameters.
Nexic Layer 6
Dark Multiverse / Lost Multiverse Aethic
Two complementary forms the Aethic multiverse takes. The Dark Multiverse is the vast array of possibility unfolding right under one’s nose — inside the same physical space as one’s own block universe, encoded informationally via the qualitative facet of the Extrusion Principle. The Lost Multiverse, by contrast, is the array of ways one’s own reality could have unfolded but did not — the counterfactual branches strung together by centric unfolding, including the many that would have held no life on Earth at all. Where the Dark Multiverse is about co-presence, the Lost Multiverse is about counterfactuality.
Aethic Layer 1
Decomposition Trichotomy Aethic
Every attribute relative to an Aethus is exactly one of: state-decomposable (fully known), blank-decomposable (fully unknown, conceptually blank), or mixed-decomposable (semiblank — some sub-attributes known, others blank). This is the formal mechanism distinguishing detector-on from detector-off.
Aethic Layer 3 · Layer 4
Dichotomy Theorem Aethic
Any attribute can be decomposed into a state-decomposable component (known) and a blank-decomposable component (unknown). The Second Postulate applies only to the unknown part. This is the principal computational tool for handling mixed-decomposable attributes.
Aethic Layer 4
Disagreeing Superposition Aethic
States that are physically copresent but cannot interact, due to nested algebraic structure that forbids interference. The appearance of classical mutual exclusivity. Formally: ∪ᵢ(Aᵢ ∩ ∩ⱼ≠ᵢ ¬Aⱼ). This is what we observe as “one or the other” in everyday life.
Aethic Layer 6
Doomsday Pacing Nexic
The exponential compression of survival timescales for island species. Applied to primates on Hațeg Island: the arrival time of theropod predators should be close to the island’s formation by Doomsday-argument reasoning, explaining why primates needed the K-Pg extinction to escape.
Nexic Layer 7
Dual-Edged Constraint Aethic
The Extrusion Principle alone is paradoxical (all outcomes coexist, yet we see one). The Union Principle alone is ad hoc (why introduce nested structure?). Together, each resolves the other’s problem. This parallels Einstein’s equivalence principle and spacetime curvature being mutually necessary.
Aethic Layer 6
E
Early-Occurrence Probability Nexic
The structured probability that an exotic-structure event — one whose intrinsic Cosmic-Nexus frequency places its expected wait at trillions of years or more — nevertheless realized within the comparatively pinpoint-minute window of the observer’s specific Aethic block-universe interval. The framework’s reading of what the flat Rare Earth probability scalars used in standard astrobiology actually are, once the two-stage lossy projection is undone.
Nexic Layer 2
EPR Resolution Aethic
The Aethic resolution of the Einstein-Podolsky-Rosen paradox. When Alice measures her particle, she extrudes to a block universe where both particles’ entire histories were always consistent with her result. No nonlocal influence occurs because no timeline is altered. Bob’s particle was always in the correlated state in Alice’s new block universe. Locality is preserved absolutely.
Aethic Layer 8
Existential Linkage Nexic
L_A = [P(H|A) − P(H|A′)] / [P(H|A) + P(H|A′)]. Measures how strongly an attribute A favors or disfavors observer existence. L_A > 0 favors, L_A < 0 disfavors, L_A = 0 means statistical independence.
Nexic Layer 8
Exotic Accordance Principle Nexic
The inferential apparatus that licenses an observer to identify exotic structure when they encounter it and to infer its anthropic role once identified. Composed of two facets that do inferentially distinct work: the Category Facet (unlikeliness implies exotic structure) and the Anthropic Facet (exotic structure implies Prerequisite). Their conjunction produces the Miracle classification via the two-step inference. The inferential complement to the Exotic Principle of Optimation’s structural claim.
Nexic Layer 2
Exotic Principle of Optimation Nexic
The structural-ontological claim Nexic reasoning makes about what kind of probabilistic object the past of an observer-supporting biosphere actually is: the natural history of an observer-supporting biosphere is a system jointly composed of common structure and exotic structure, with any and all instances of exotic structure almost surely traceable to co-existing with a Miracle. The Miracle is the probabilistic object so far removed in likelihood of occurrence on timescales near billions of years that its existing renders it categorically separate from the universe’s normal constituents. Load-bearing for the formal Miracle classification and the structural ground of Layer 2.
Nexic Layer 2
Exotic Structure Nexic
Events whose intrinsic Cosmic-Nexus hazard rate is combinatorially slower than their realized frequency in the observer’s biosphere — events the universe would essentially never produce in any window it has had, yet which have happened in the observer’s specific natural history. The K-Pg extinction, the eukaryotic merger, the α-gal crisis, and the Hațeg refuge structure are the framework’s canonical instances. Paired with common structure as the two regimes the Exotic Principle names; treated by the framework as “imported” into the observer’s block-universe past by Optimation.
Nexic Layer 2
Extrusion Principle Aethic
When an observer gains information, they do not collapse a wavefunction within their current universe. They transition to a different block universe whose entire history is consistent with the new knowledge. The particle never changed; the observer’s Aethus did. The prior block universe retains its superposition indefinitely.
Aethic Layer 1
Extrusion-Consequential Principle Aethic
When an observer extrudes to a new block universe, the entire history of that universe—past, present, and future—was always consistent with the new information. The past changes not because it is rewritten but because the observer now occupies a different complete spacetime. This is why retrocausality appears in delayed-choice experiments without actually violating causality.
Aethic Layer 1
F
First Postulate Aethic
Any attribution of realism is a statement of relation to a particular Aethus. There is no observer-independent “view from nowhere.” This is an ontological claim, not epistemic humility: the Aethus is the fundamental unit of reality.
Aethic Layer 2
Fundamental Theorem of Aethic Reasoning Aethic
To every valid Aethic uncertainty principle there corresponds an Aethic coherence principle. Wherever information is permanently hidden, possible states interact as if physically coexisting. This generalizes quantum coherence to all domains.
Aethic Layer 9
Fundamental Theorem of Empirical Aethae Aethic
Writing down one’s own Aethus yields white noise: the act of empirically specifying your own informational state is self-defeating because the specification itself changes the state. This is the Aethic analogue of “the Tao that can be spoken is not the true Tao.”
Aethic Layer 2
Fundamental Theorem of Nexic Reasoning Nexic
Given an attentor (observer-selection Nexus) and a contentor (cosmic baseline Nexus) satisfying five conditions including statistical independence and mediocrity, an Aethus disjoint from a mediocre one is almost surely vastly less probable under the contentor. The engine that converts Accordance Principle bounds into quantitative predictions.
Nexic Layer 3
H
Hațeg Island Nexic
A Late Cretaceous island in what is now Romania, proposed as the site where early primates survived theropod predation pressure due to insular isolation. The Nexic prediction: primate fossils should be found in the Hațeg deposits. This would constitute direct evidence for the framework. Doomsday pacing constrains how long they could have survived before theropod arrival.
Nexic Layer 7
High-Contrast Earth Nexic
Minute changes in Earth’s stated-attributes cascade to orders-of-magnitude shifts in observer probability. The mechanism is multiplicative compound probability (the Sisyphean Trap): each fix to make an alternative history habitable demands further fixes, and the product collapses toward zero.
Nexic Layer 4
Hyperblock Universe Aethic
The Markov chain of block universes viewed as a single object. Reality in Aethic reasoning is not one block universe but a chain of them, each a complete relativistic 4D spacetime, with transitions between nodes driven by information gain. The hyperblock is the full chain. Resolves the tension between the static block universe (compatible with relativity but no temporal flow) and the growing block universe (has temporal flow but conflicts with relativity).
Aethic Layer 1
Hyperrealizable / Realizable Probability Aethic
Two definitions of probability in Aethic reasoning. Realizable probability counts only valid Aethae (matching physical observables). Hyperrealizable probability also includes invalid Aethae with nonzero weights. Analysis is often performed over hyperrealizable probabilities and projected to realizable probabilities at the end—this is the proposed mechanism underlying Bayesian inference.
Aethic Layer 5
I
IMD Nameability Principle Nexic
The structural property of Imperfection Magnitude Drops concerning the asymmetry between the numerosity of articulable Aethae and the population of high-probability Aethae. The more extreme the IMD, the more numerous the abstract natural histories that can be descriptively articulated at that probability tier — meaning any specific Aethus articulated as an alternative to our own is overwhelmingly likely to be a sure IMD, not because the high-probability vicinity is empty but because articulation operates over a population concentrated almost entirely on the IMDs. The Accordance Principle resolves the asymmetry from the other side, delivering peak content from the inside.
Nexic Layer 7
Imperfection Magnitude Drop (IMD) Nexic
Any targeted perturbation away from one’s own Aethus where the probability of observer existence becomes drastically minute. IMDs form a continuous spectrum by probabilistic severity — a gamma-ray burst and a red-dwarf scenario differ only in magnitude, not in kind.
Nexic Layer 4
Indiscriminate Time Principle Aethic
Uncertainty fans out into the past just as it does into the future. The ontological nature of not-knowing is the same in both temporal directions. A medieval battle whose outcome you don’t know is in superposition relative to your Aethus right now.
Aethic Layer 1
Inference Case / Null Case Nexic
The two cases the licensing argument of Leg 1 partitions historical improbable events into. In the inference case, the event prompts structural inference — the observer’s Aethus contains some feature preventing the event from having been a mere fluke. In the null case, the event is accepted as a genuine fluke and no inference is licensed. The Indiscriminate Time Principle’s contribution is to make the null case inaccessible for retrospectively-learned past events, forcing the inference case obtains.
Nexic Layer 3
Inheritance Paradox Aethic
The central obstacle in deriving the Third Postulate. If contradictions in a child Aethus propagate back to the parent Aethus via simple single-generational rules, then contradictions propagate between any two Aethae — which would force an objective reality and violate the First Postulate. The Third Postulate's two-generational quantifier structure (∃B, ∀C) is precisely what resolves the paradox while preserving relational ontology. Encountered on a soccer field in August 2022.
Aethic Layer 7
Intermediate-Region Emptiness Nexic
The empirical prediction that intermediate-regime objects — events at moderately exotic but not deeply exotic hazard rates — are almost absent in the observer’s biosphere. Squeezed from both sides: the common-structure perturbation pool has cosmically low probability of reaching the intermediate region; the exotic-structure regime’s Optimal-Earth corollary makes identifying any intermediate-regime exotic event almost impossibly difficult. Events tend to discretely skip the intermediate region, presenting either as common structure or as deep-exotic Miracle-class structure.
Nexic Layer 2
Intrinsic Frequency Principle Nexic
The empirical inferential prerequisite that motivates the Exotic Principle: natural history broadly provides empirical events whose intrinsic Cosmic-Nexus frequencies are drastically lower than the frequencies with which they actually occurred in the observer’s biosphere. The empirical bridge between the Optimation Fallacy (the negative warning that probabilistic mediocrity in our biosphere is not reflective of objective mediocrity) and the Exotic Principle (the positive structural claim that some events live in an entirely different probabilistic regime).
Nexic Layer 2
L
Ladder Ontology Aethic
The structural form that the Union Principle takes: disagreeing-superposition states are layered into nested "pockets" that share the same spatial substrate but cannot causally interact. Each observer lives in one specific pocket determined by their accumulated Aethic information. Both unions and intersections manifest identically as physical AND, yet their algebraic nesting preserves logical distinctions while maintaining a single phenomenological degree of freedom — packing more structure into the same physical space than classical ontology recognized.
Aethic Layer 6
Leg 1 / Leg 2 Nexic
The two stages of the Accordance Principle’s derivation. Leg 1 is the licensing step: the Indiscriminate Time Principle dissolves the null case for retrospectively-learned past events, forcing the observer to infer that some structural feature of their Aethus prevented the event from having been a fluke. Leg 2 is the quantitative step: Aethic Mediocrity, applied at the parent-Aethus partition Leg 1’s output identifies, converts the bare structural-inference demand into the anthropic-necessity-shaped Bayesian bound that constitutes the General Form of the Accordance Principle.
Nexic Layer 3
M
Miracle Nexic
The formal unit of Nexic reasoning: an Aethus satisfying both the cosmic-rarity criterion (phenomenal unlikelihood under the Cosmic Nexus) and the anthropic-prerequisite criterion (non-negligible probability under the Aetherian Nexus). A genuinely distinct ontological class. The K-Pg extinction, the GGTA1 gene loss in primate ancestors, and the Chicxulub impactor's size and composition are each Miracles — vanishingly unlikely absent anthropic selection, effectively required given observer existence. Nexic Layer 3
P
Permanencible Attribute Aethic
A portmanteau of "permanently accessible." An attribute which, when added to a particular Aethus, triggers invalidity by so much as even stating another attribute in question within that same Aethus. The derivational motivation was to make permanently blank attributes well-defined in the same way that an open set is well-defined in topology — not by defining them as a primitive, but by grounding them in the external structure of the Aethus (which became the Third Postulate). Three common types: (1) a conditional attribute explicitly stating that another attribute is blank (the Heisenberg case), (2) a set-constraint attribute that locks the remaining possibilities so that specifying one would contradict the set (Tim’s dog breed), and (3) a probability-fixing attribute whose stated value would be destroyed by resolving the Boolean it describes (tomorrow’s rain).

Aethic Layer 7
Reduced Form Coupling Aethic
The attribute-level correlation that emerges as a side-effect of the reduced form's pruning. When two or more attributes could otherwise combine freely, but certain joint configurations would produce downstream contradictions, the Third Postulate eliminates those configurations — and the survivors are coupled, because their permitted values now depend on each other. No signal, no hidden common cause: the coupling is the structural residue of validity-preserving pruning. A single procedural operation appearing across quantum entanglement, delayed-choice experiments, the multi-slit powerset classification, Wigner's friend and Frauchiger–Renner consistency, and the Newcomb coin-flip resolution — the only differences being the coupled attributes and the weights.
