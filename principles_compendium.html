Ajax Benander—Compendium of Concepts

The Aethic–Nexic
Framework

A filterable encyclopedia of the principles, postulates, theorems, definitions, thought experiments, and mathematical objects that constitute Ajax Benander’s solution to the quantum measurement problem and his program of anthropic reasoning over natural history.

Tiers: S / A / B / C / D Frameworks: Aethic · Nexic · Golden · Both · MetaAxes: tier = dependency importance · kind = what the entry is · evidence = what supports it Updated: October 2026 — claim statements reconciled with the current paper versions; entry-level revision dates to follow

The operational criterion for placement is how much of the framework would collapse if this item were removed? Tier S contains irreducible axioms; Tier A contains load-bearing structural principles; Tier B contains major supporting principles and mathematical infrastructure; Tier C contains supporting definitions, operations, and interpretations; Tier D contains highly specific empirical postulates, lemmas, and narrow derivations.

Ajax has written that he “literally had not named any of the principles until late 2024 while trying to put arbitrary fences in the framework for needed pedagogical effectiveness.” Dates track conceptual discovery, not formal naming. Both frameworks were conceptually complete by mid-2023; the formal papers were published in late 2024.

Use the filter bar to cut the content down to what you want to see. Each filter group supports multi-select: clicking “all” resets the group, clicking any individual button switches to selecting just that one, and additional clicks add more items to the active set. Clicking an active item again removes it (and reverts to “all” if nothing remains selected). The “both” button under Framework is special—it filters strictly to entries that span both Aethic and Nexic frameworks, encoded as entries carrying both tags simultaneously rather than a separate “both” tag. All filter groups compose with AND logic across groups.
