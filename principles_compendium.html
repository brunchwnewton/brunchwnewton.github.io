The complete catalogue of Aethic and Nexic reasoning — more than five hundred principles, theorems, definitions, and thought experiments, tiered from the irreducible core outward and filterable by domain.

567 entries 5 tiers 8 domains

This is a map, not the territory. The framework’s full development lives in the papers; what follows is a structured index of everything in them, so a specific idea can be located and placed relative to the rest.

How to read this catalogue

The irreducible core and the load-bearing principles carry full entries. The three larger tiers — hundreds of definitions, supporting concepts, and narrow derivations — are given as a searchable, filterable title index, each tagged by domain, since their full statements belong to the papers. One entry, the billiard-ball experiment, is flagged reframed: the current papers treat it as an idealized limiting regime and open construction, not the decisive near-term test an earlier version described.
